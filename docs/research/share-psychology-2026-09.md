# What travelers are proud to share — and how the Virtual Passport capitalizes on it

**Research, 2026-09-29.** Commissioned by the founder after the pivot to Passport + nearby finders
(commit 99a2dd8, docs/PLANS.md). The question, in the founder's words: *"what people are proud of
sharing, what people are proud of taking a picture of when they travel"* — including local travel
(the founder's own Atlanta posts: shrimp & grits, catfish, buttermilk fried chicken, whale shark
videos) — and how GlobeSkimmers becomes *"the number one social media in the virtual passport
stamping niche."*

Every claim marked **[evidence]** has a cited source. Claims marked **[hypothesis]** are our
inference — plausible, not proven. Do not quote a hypothesis as a fact to partners or press.

---

## Executive summary — 10 bullets

1. **People share travel to build an identity, not an archive.** Research on tourist photo-sharing
   finds self-presentation is the primary motive, ahead of memory-keeping; among younger travelers
   the photo exists *in order to be shared*. Our share image is therefore the product, not a
   feature. [evidence]
2. **Food is the #1 local-travel share** because it is the only travel-class experience available
   at home: 51% of travelers pick where to eat from social media, 45% of Americans call friends'
   food photos the most influential restaurant factor, and food/dining is the top travel activity
   people post about even before the trip. The founder's Atlanta food posts are the norm, not the
   exception. [evidence]
3. **"Brag with proof" is a proven mechanic.** Strava's culture ("if it's not on Strava, it didn't
   happen") shows GPS proof turns a boast into a credential; the marathon medal shows a guaranteed,
   effort-independent token of completion is what actually gets photographed. Our GPS-verified ✓
   is the digital medal — frame it as *earned*, never as surveillance. [evidence]
4. **Collecting beats streaks for travel.** The National Parks passport (40 years, 3,000+ free
   stamps, a national club scene) and Japan's eki stamps (since 1931, 9,000+ stations) prove
   set-collection sustains decades of engagement with zero daily obligation. Duolingo-style streaks
   generate measurable anxiety and churn; even Duolingo's own data shows leniency *raises* DAU.
   The founder's standing "no streaks, no guilt" rule is evidence-aligned. [evidence]
5. **The share image is the growth engine.** Wordle went from ~90 to 300,000 daily players when its
   copy-paste emoji grid — a spoiler-free humblebrag built from observed user behavior — went
   viral. TikTok and Strava run the watermark + deferred-deep-link version of the same loop. Our
   platform-preset share images (already built) need a watermark install path and per-share install
   attribution. [evidence]
6. **The next three stamp lanes, by share-evidence: food, wildlife, summits.** Famous-dish stamps
   at institutions people travel *for*; creature stamps at aquariums/zoos (the founder's whale
   shark video = the only whale sharks outside Asia — the exclusivity fact IS the brag); GPS-at-
   elevation summit stamps (highest effort = highest brag, hardest to fake). [evidence + hypothesis]
7. **Event stamps are the ticket stub reborn.** Mobile ticketing killed the physical stub; a
   GPS-verified "I was in this building for this event, this date" stamp replaces it. It is a
   Passport feature and does not need the removed events surface — only gating data. [hypothesis,
   built on evidenced medal/proof psychology]
8. **LA28 is reachable without touching a single protected mark.** The Ted Stevens Act gives USOPC
   near-absolute rights over "Olympic," the rings, and "Team USA" — no confusion showing needed,
   and they sue (Prime Hydration, 2024). But *venue + sport + date + city + matchup* are facts,
   the same nominative-facts doctrine our film stamps already use. Dress rehearsals exist on the
   calendar: Super Bowl LXI at SoFi (Feb 14, 2027) and the Women's World Cup in Brazil (Jun 24 –
   Jul 25, 2027). [evidence]
9. **"Virtual passport" is ownable — but "GPS-verified" alone is not.** The niche is a scatter of
   2023–2025 micro-apps (Stampie, Stamped, PASSPRT, CountryLogs), several already claiming GPS
   verification, almost all country/city-level. Nobody owns the phrase, the place-level catalog
   (our 18,654 stampable places), the art, or the social layer. The EU's Entry/Exit System ending
   physical stamping across 29 countries is the press hook of the decade for this niche. [evidence]
10. **First 10k users come from dense scenes, not broadcasts.** BeReal (campus ambassadors →
    App of the Year), Strava (existing clubs get an online home), Untappd (0→3M on guerilla + one
    press moment), Polarsteps (20M on word of mouth) all won by activating communities that already
    existed. Ours: Atlanta first (the founder's own story is the first-run explainer), then
    national-park collectors, study-abroad cohorts, and the friend-tag "claim your stamp" loop
    already in the build queue. [evidence for their playbooks; hypothesis for our mapping]

## 1. What travel content people actually share, and why

### 1.1 The motives, per published research

- A Tourism Management Perspectives study identifies four tourist photo-sharing motives:
  **enjoyment, altruism, self-expression, social relationship** — and finds self-centered motives
  (staying present online, self-expression) are "an essential part of travel sharing."
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1447677022000900 [evidence]
- Frontiers in Psychology (2022) work on travel photo sharing: **self-presentation and impression
  management are primary motivators**; tourists strategically edit and compose to manage
  impressions. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.838176/full [evidence]
- Among younger travelers, **the main purpose of taking photos is not to preserve memories but to
  share them** — the share is the point of the photo.
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9024168/ (context in the same literature) [evidence]
- Sharing feeds back into satisfaction: tourists who share photos **evaluate the trip itself more
  positively**, directly and via pleasure. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9024168/ [evidence]
- 72% of people post photos **while still on vacation**; ~97% of Millennials post while traveling.
  https://passport-photo.online/blog/social-media-vs-travel/ ·
  https://photoaid.com/blog/how-social-media-affects-travel-statistics/ [evidence, industry survey grade]

**Product translation [hypothesis]:** the Passport must be shareable *in the moment, from the
place* — which is exactly what the GPS-verified nearby-stamp prompt already does. The share
action belongs inside the stamping moment, not in a later "export" menu.

### 1.2 The top shared categories

Across platform surveys: **landmarks** dominate during the trip, **food/dining** dominates before
and around it, and both are decision-driving for the audience.
https://www.webfx.com/blog/social-media/social-media-mobile-travel/ [evidence]

The proof-of-accomplishment genre (medals, summit signs, finish lines) is its own category — see
1.5. Wildlife is a perennial engagement class (see the whale shark note in section 2). Sunsets are
high-volume but commodity: they carry no identity information, which is why we should not build a
"sunset stamp" — anyone can post a sunset; only a *credential* is scarce. [hypothesis]

### 1.3 Why FOOD is the #1 local-travel share

- **51%** of travelers said their decision of where/what to eat while traveling came from social
  media; **45%** of Americans call friends' social images the most influential factor in choosing
  a restaurant at a destination; **57%+** have picked a restaurant from its photos/videos.
  https://www.fattiretours.com/news/survey-reveals-how-travelers-use-social-media-2018/ [evidence]
- Foodstagramming research: sharing food photos **enhances the eating experience itself** (the
  photo functions like a pre-eating ritual that increases enjoyment) and serves status, uniqueness,
  self-esteem and favorable self-presentation.
  https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.634261/full ·
  https://www.socialmediatoday.com/social-networks/psychology-foodstagramming [evidence]
- Food creates intimacy: "food is a universal language, and sharing it helps us bond."
  https://www.socialmediatoday.com/social-networks/psychology-foodstagramming [evidence]

**Why it dominates *local* sharing [hypothesis, consistent with the evidence]:** food is the only
travel-class experience that needs no trip. You cannot summit a peak in your lunch hour, but you
can eat the city's iconic dish and post it — claiming local insider identity ("I know where the
real shrimp & grits is") rather than tourist identity. It is cheap, repeatable weekly, and
photographs well at phone distance. The founder in Atlanta — not a tourist, proudly posting
shrimp & grits, catfish, buttermilk fried chicken — is the textbook case. **A passport that only
stamps trips misses the person's weekly proud moment; a food lane captures it.**

### 1.4 The psychology of collecting — and why the two great stamp programs work

- **Endowed progress effect** (Nunes & Drèze): people given artificial head-start progress toward
  a goal persist harder and complete more; a set shown as "2 of 10 collected" outperforms "8 to
  go." https://www.researchgate.net/publication/23547282_The_Endowed_Progress_Effect_How_Artificial_Advancement_Increases_Effort [evidence]
- **Goal-gradient effect**: effort rises as the goal nears — the last stamps in a set pull hardest.
  https://learningloop.io/plays/psychology/goal-gradient-effect [evidence]
- **National Parks passport (USA, since 1986):** free rubber cancellation stamps at 400+ NPS units,
  3,000+ official cancellations, "wildly successful," with clubs, websites and events adding
  "competition and storytelling." The book costs money; the stamps are free — people pay for the
  *container* of a free collection.
  https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/ ·
  https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/passport-stamps.htm [evidence]
- **Eki stamps (Japan, since 1931):** free station stamps, scaled to 1,400 stations by the 1970
  "Discover Japan" campaign after Expo '70, now a 9,000-station quest with a devoted collector
  culture (*oshi-tetsu*) and themed limited-time **stamp rallies** (e.g. JR East's Pokémon rally)
  that manufacture urgency without daily obligation. A digital version (EKITAG) already exists.
  https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/practical-guide-to-collecting-japans-eki-train-station-stamps ·
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eki_stamp · https://www.japanhousela.com/articles/station-to-station-japans-iconic-eki-stamps-train-stamp-book-passport-goshuin-goshuincho/ [evidence]

**What both programs teach [evidence-backed reading]:** (a) the stamp is *place-locked* — you must
physically go, which is exactly our GPS gate; (b) the collection is *episodic* — no decay, no
daily duty, a 10-year gap costs you nothing; (c) *sets and rallies* (a region's parks, a line's
stations, a summer campaign) provide the goal-gradient pull; (d) the artifact is *beautiful* —
people photograph the stamped page itself, which validates the founder's stamp-art investment
(PLANS.md queue item 2).

### 1.5 Streaks vs badges — and the founder's "no streaks/guilt" rule

- Duolingo's streaks retain hard (a 7-day streak → 3.6× course completion) **but** the literature
  and reporting document streak *anxiety*: loss aversion overtakes joy, users perform empty
  actions to protect the number, and quitting often follows a broken streak.
  https://trophy.so/blog/the-psychology-of-streaks-how-sylvi-weaponized-duolingos-best-feature-against-them ·
  https://www.fastcompany.com/91004057/the-duolingo-effect-how-keeping-the-streak-is-changing-the-way-people-behave [evidence]
- Duolingo's own finding: **being more lenient with streaks increased engagement** — streak
  freezes made DAU rise. https://yukaichou.com/gamification-study/master-the-art-of-streak-design-for-short-term-engagement-and-long-term-success/ [evidence]
- Travel is episodic by nature; a daily streak punishes the actual cadence of trips. Badges/sets
  reward whenever the behavior happens. **The founder's standing rule (Google of Travel brief,
  2026-09-02: anticipation loop, "NO streaks/guilt") is the right call and is now evidence-backed;
  keep it.** Use *sets* (endowed progress + goal gradient) and *time-boxed rallies* (eki model)
  for urgency instead. [evidence + founder decision]

### 1.6 "Brag with proof" — the ✓ is the medal

- Strava's culture is literally named for proof: **"If it's not on Strava, it didn't happen."**
  The GPS record "serves as proof for bragging rights — something verbal boasting alone cannot
  provide"; qualitative research on Strava users finds self-presentation, social pressure, and
  motivation as the three perceived psychosocial themes.
  https://www.welovecycling.com/wide/2025/01/30/strava-or-it-didnt-happen-the-psychology-of-digital-bragging-for-cyclists/ ·
  https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/%E2%80%9CIf-It's-not-on-Strava-it-Didn%E2%80%99t-Happen%E2%80%9D:-Perceived-Russell-Potts/58f3d1b7aa6e2e82613ee9f0f6c819d9dbdf8f3a [evidence]
- The marathon **finisher medal** shows the other half: a guaranteed token of *completion*
  (independent of time or rank) is what gets photographed and posted — ~51% of runners share the
  race experience, and sharing feeds future motivation.
  https://mkmarathon.com/what-is-finishers-medal-why-it-matters-runners/ ·
  https://www.runifyapp.com/blog/race-medal-statistics [evidence]

**How to frame our GPS ✓ [recommendation]:** it is an *earned mark*, never a police check. Copy
direction: "Verified — I was really here," the ✓ engraved into the stamp (top-left, as already
built), and share captions that lead with the credential ("GPS-verified at the Georgia Aquarium").
Two cautions: (1) never shame the unverified — a self-stamped memory is still a memory; the ✓ is
a *bonus tier*, exactly as the three-proof ladder (gps > photo EXIF > photo AI > photo > self)
already treats it; (2) in marketing, "you can't fake it" reads as a challenge — prefer "no one can
doubt it," which is the Strava sentiment verbatim. Note honestly: small competitors (Stamped,
PASSPRT) also market "GPS-verified" — the ✓ alone is not the moat; the ✓ *plus* art, place-level
catalog, and the social layer is (see 4.4). [hypothesis]

### 1.7 The Wordle lesson: the share artifact is the growth engine

Wordle grew from ~90 daily players (Nov 2021) to ~300,000 (late Dec 2021) when the emoji-grid
share went viral — a mechanic Wardle *copied from observed user behavior* (New Zealand players
hand-typing colored squares), spoiler-free, instantly legible, and perfectly shaped for a
humblebrag. https://www.aakashg.com/wordle/ ·
https://beastoftraal.com/2022/01/04/wordles-viral-marketing-tactic-makes-brilliant-use-of-people-as-media/ [evidence]

**Our equivalents already exist** (branded share image, per-platform presets, carousel posts).
What's missing is the loop's return path: watermark → tap → install → claim (section 4.2).

## 2. Product moves: which stamp lanes to add next

Ranked by the sharing evidence above. Each lane must clear the founder's absolute fame bar
(stamp-worthiness philosophy: world/national/regional icon or event-gated; local spots never
stamp) — these lanes *extend the bar's scopes*, they do not lower it.

### 2.1 Food stamps — "I ate the dish" (top priority of the three)

- **Why it will be shared:** food is the #1 local share (1.3); a dish stamp gives the weekly food
  post a credential and a collection ("3 of Atlanta's 7 icon dishes"). It makes GlobeSkimmers a
  *local* habit, not a twice-a-year trip app — the founder's own Atlanta behavior is the target
  behavior. [evidence for sharing; hypothesis for retention effect]
- **What the stamp shows:** dish name + institution + city + date + "I ate it here!" and the
  GPS ✓; the traveler's own food photo leads, photo-first layout (already built for scene stamps).
  Typographic first; dish art later (a bowl of pho, a plate of grits — our own art, no rights
  issues).
- **The fame bar, food edition [needs a founder decision]:** we already stamp restaurants' *cities*;
  a dish stamp is a new kind and must not open the door to every restaurant. Proposed bar, parallel
  to the existing scopes: institutions people *travel for* (Katz's Deli, Franklin Barbecue, Café du
  Monde, Pike Place original Starbucks-tier fame) and **pride-of-city dishes** at their canonical
  houses (Atlanta: shrimp & grits, fried chicken at the historic institutions; Philly cheesesteak
  at the two rivals; Chicago deep dish; Nashville hot chicken; New Orleans beignets). Free signals
  exist as with places: Wikipedia pageviews of the dish + the institution, James Beard "America's
  Classics," decades-in-business. Seed ~150–300 curated spots, founder-reviewed, US-first.
  [hypothesis + open decision]
- **Riskiness: LOW legal, MEDIUM taste.** Dish names and restaurant names are facts (same
  nominative-use doctrine as the film stamps: plain text, no logos, never "official"). Taste risk
  is curation drift — a mediocre spot with a stamp reads as an ad; the fame bar and founder review
  protect against it. One flag: do not accept payment for food stamps while calling them icons
  (honest-UX principle); paid placement, if ever, must be labeled.

### 2.2 Wildlife / creature stamps at aquariums, zoos, sanctuaries

- **Why it will be shared:** animals are a top engagement class, and the founder's own whale shark
  videos are the proof case. Crucially, the brag is often an *exclusivity fact*: Georgia Aquarium
  is the only aquarium outside Asia — the only place in the Western Hemisphere — with whale sharks.
  https://www.georgiaaquarium.org/resource-center/stories/20-years-of-wonder-georgia-aquariums-impact-on-whale-sharks/ [evidence]
  The stamp should *say the fact*: "I met the whale sharks — the only ones outside Asia."
- **What the stamp shows:** the creature (our own art — animals are not protectable; the founder's
  queue item 2 "Stone Mountain class" art applies), venue name as fact, city, date, ✓. Optionally
  a per-species series inside one venue (whale shark / beluga / manta) → set mechanics inside a
  single visit, which also drives return visits.
- **Riskiness: LOW legal with own art** (venue name = fact; NEVER the aquarium's logo or mascot
  art without a deal — PLANS.md item 2 already flags licensed partner art needs permission).
  MEDIUM reputational: captive-animal sentiment runs hot in some audiences (the AJC has covered
  Georgia Aquarium's image challenges: https://www.ajc.com/news/georgia-aquarium-faces-challenges-its-image-marks-years/lVxzLI0LBUGPGup5hntNBM/).
  Mitigation: include sanctuaries and wild-viewing sites (whale watching, monarch groves) so the
  lane reads "creatures," not "captivity." [evidence + judgment]
- **Partnership upside:** this is the cleanest lane for the monetization research (queue item 3) —
  an aquarium gets a shareable, GPS-verified visitor badge for free; ask for official art rights,
  a stamper discount, or a per-stamp fee. Georgia Aquarium first, since the founder's visit
  already produced the stamp flow. [hypothesis]

### 2.3 Summit / trail stamps

- **Why it will be shared:** the summit-sign photo is an established genre, and effort is the
  currency of the brag — the medal literature (1.5) shows completion tokens for hard things are
  the most-photographed objects. GPS at a summit is also the *strongest* possible use of our
  verification: high effort, hard to fake, and the ✓ genuinely adds information (anyone can post
  a photo *of* a mountain). [evidence for medal psychology; hypothesis for transfer]
- **What the stamp shows:** peak/trail name + elevation + date + ✓ ("Half Dome · 8,839 ft ·
  I made it!"); the traveler's summit photo leads, photo-first layout. Natural wonders are already
  "always in" under the fame bar, so much of the seed (Half Dome, Angels Landing, Camino
  waypoints, 14ers, Diamond Head) is bar-consistent.
- **Riskiness: LOW legal, REAL safety/taste.** Do not gamify danger: no summit leaderboards, no
  time rankings, no "first to stamp," and consider the stamp valid from the trailhead-to-summit
  corridor rather than the exact exposed point. NPS trademark note: park names in plain text are
  fine; the NPS arrowhead logo is protected — never draw it. [judgment]
- **Audience synergy:** this lane is the direct digital sibling of the National Parks passport —
  its collector community (clubs, blogs, events: https://americasnationalparks.org/passport-to-your-national-parks/)
  is a named acquisition scene in section 4.

### 2.4 Event stamps

Covered fully in section 3 — they are the fourth lane, with the strongest calendar hook (LA28)
and the strictest rights constraints.

### 2.5 Explicitly deprioritized

- **Race/marathon stamps:** the psychology is proven (1.5) but race names are aggressively
  enforced trademarks (the B.A.A. polices "Boston Marathon") and races already hand out a
  physical medal — we'd be second trophy, not first. Revisit as an event-stamp subtype ("Marathon
  day in Boston · date · ✓ on the course") if demand shows. [judgment]
- **Sunset/scenery stamps:** no identity information, no scarcity — see 1.2. [hypothesis]

## 3. Event stamps and the LA 2028 plan

### 3.1 The product: the ticket stub reborn

Mobile ticketing killed the stub that generations kept as proof of "I was there." A GPS-verified
stamp — **"I was in this arena · USA vs Brazil · July 22, 2028"** — is the stub's replacement,
with better proof than the stub ever had. This is a **Passport feature, not a listings feature**:
the events *surface* was removed in the pivot (99a2dd8), but event stamps only need gating data
(is there an event at this venue today?), which the worker can get from the Ticketmaster API
(`TICKETMASTER_API_KEY` is already a worker secret) with no user-facing listings UI. The founder's
rule already exists in the stamp-worthiness doctrine: stadiums/arenas stamp ONLY with a same-day
event — "Watched a game at" + matchup + date, our typography, no team logos. [founder decision +
architecture fact; stub framing is hypothesis]

### 3.2 The legal reality (why the compliant design matters)

- The **Ted Stevens Act** grants USOPC exclusive commercial rights to the five rings, the word
  "Olympic," and any indicator of association with the USOPC — and uniquely, USOPC **need not
  prove consumer confusion** to win. https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/usopc-ramping-up-trademark-enforcement-as-paris-summer-olympics-approach ·
  https://natlawreview.com/article/keeping-flame-alive-how-ip-protects-legacy-olympics-decathalaw-series-article-10 [evidence]
- They enforce: USOPC sued Prime Hydration over "Olympic," "Olympian," "Team USA," and "Going for
  Gold" on packaging/ads. https://natlawreview.com/article/us-olympic-paralympic-committee-suing-us-beverage-company-over-trademark [evidence]
- Enforcement historically **ramps up before each Games**; law firms are already advising brands
  to prepare for 2028. https://www.spencerfane.com/insight/never-too-early-olympic-games-2028-preparation-part-i-intellectual-property-issues/ [evidence]

### 3.3 The compliant stamp (build this regardless of any partnership)

**Never on any surface (stamps, share images, App Store copy, ads, push copy):** the rings, torch
imagery, "Olympic"/"Olympiad"/"Olympian," "Team USA," "Going for Gold," "Road to LA28," "LA28,"
medal-podium iconography, or a five-color ring-evoking motif. **Safe, because they are facts used
nominatively** (the exact doctrine our film-scene stamps already established: titles, names, dates
as plain-text facts, no logos, never "official"):

> **SoFi Stadium** · Swimming · July 23, 2028 · Inglewood, CA · "I was there!" · ✓
> **Crypto.com Arena** · Gymnastics · July 19, 2028 · Los Angeles · "I was there!" · ✓
> **BMO Stadium** · Soccer · USA vs Brazil · July 20, 2028 · "I watched it live!" · ✓

Venue names are third-party marks used truthfully to name the place (as we already do for every
stamped attraction); sport names are generic; matchups by *country name* ("USA vs Brazil") are
facts — but never the phrase "Team USA." Use our own generic sport silhouettes, not LA28's
pictograms (those are copyrighted works). Marketing copy says "summer 2028 in Los Angeles," never
"the Olympics." Have a lawyer sanity-check the final template once, before the 2027 test events.
[evidence for the rules; the template is our design]

**Key context for the games:** July 14–30, 2028; opening ceremony shared between the LA Memorial
Coliseum and SoFi Stadium; swimming at SoFi (38,000 seats), athletics at the Coliseum week one,
gymnastics and boxing finals at Crypto.com Arena. Venue list is public now — pre-seed every venue.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venues_of_the_2028_Summer_Olympics_and_Paralympics ·
https://www.olympics.com/en/news/la28-olympic-games-venues-host-cities [evidence]

### 3.4 The partnership version (optional, never load-bearing)

An **official licensee** path exists: USOPC/LA28 take merchandise-licensee proposals at
licensing@TEAMUSA2028.ORG, judged on product, distribution, CSR compliance, and licensing track
record. https://docket.acc.com/node/3971 · https://www.usopc.org/commercial-and-brand-usage-guidelines [evidence]
What it would buy: the rings/wordmark on stamps, "official" designation, possible in-venue
presence. What it costs: royalties + minimum guarantees, long lead, and category conflict risk —
Fanatics already holds a sweeping LA28/Team USA merchandising deal
(https://frontofficesports.com/fanatics-strikes-merchandising-deal-with-la28-team-usa/), and
whether "digital collectibles/app" is open is unknown. **Recommendation:** ship the compliant
factual version on our own power; send an exploratory licensee inquiry mid-2027 *only if* event
stamps show traction — and price the answer against the fact that the factual version already
works. [evidence + judgment]

## 4. Network playbook — the first 10,000 engaged users

### 4.1 What worked for the comparables [all evidence]

- **BeReal:** a year of quiet French campus growth to 500K, then a US/UK **college ambassador
  program** (Jan 2022): $30–50 per verified install early on, then parties, greek-life and student-
  org partnerships and student-paper placements under a hired field-marketing lead; free party
  entry for "download + add five friends." Finished 2022 as iPhone App of the Year.
  https://research.contrary.com/company/bereal · https://read.first1000.co/p/bereal [evidence]
- **Strava:** cyclist-first niche obsession (accurate Segments), then running (3× the market), and
  **clubs** — giving already-existing real-world communities an online home so every club pulls
  its members in; growth ran on in-app sharing, not ad spend.
  https://community.inc/deep-dives/community-growth-strava · https://nogood.io/blog/strava-marketing-strategy/ [evidence]
- **Untappd:** built in weeks, **0 → 3M users on guerilla marketing with no ad budget**; one
  Mashable article took DAU 100 → 5,000 in a day; badges did the retention (1B check-ins, 1B
  badges by 2021–22). https://wineandmore.com/stories/10-years-of-untappd-beer-drinking/ ·
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untappd [evidence]
- **Polarsteps:** 1M (2019) → **20M travelers**, "largely driven by word of mouth rather than paid
  marketing" — product quality as the growth strategy.
  https://news.polarsteps.com/news/20-million-travelers-and-counting-tech-success-story-polarsteps-celebrates-its-fast-growing-community [evidence]
- **Wordle:** the share artifact as the entire engine (1.7). [evidence]

**The common shape:** a dense scene that already exists + an artifact members are proud to show +
zero-friction spread. None of the five bought their first users with ads (BeReal's bounties were
scene-activation, not broadcast).

### 4.2 The mechanics that fit stamps

1. **Share image → install loop (the engine).** Every share render carries the wordmark +
   globeskimmers.io (already true) **plus a deferred deep link** so a tap on the shared
   story/post lands in the store and, post-install, on *that stamp's* claim/view page — the
   TikTok/Strava watermark pattern with per-share attribution.
   https://www.tapp.so/blog/social-viral-loops-for-apps/ [evidence for the pattern]
   The admin stamped-vs-shared table already counts shares; extend it with installs-per-share so
   we know which stamp types actually convert. **This is move #1 — everything else multiplies it.**
2. **Tag-a-friend = "claim your stamp."** Already in the build queue (tagged friend is offered the
   stamp). Make the *share* the invite: "Maiza stamped the Georgia Aquarium with you — claim your
   stamp" is an invitation with a concrete gift attached, not a generic referral. Wordle's lesson:
   the invite should feel like showing, not selling. [founder plan + hypothesis]
3. **City sets, not streaks.** "7 of Atlanta's 10 icons" progress on the passport page and on the
   share image (the endowed-progress framing: always show what's collected, count from >0 for
   anyone with ≥1 stamp). Time-boxed **rallies** (eki model: "Atlanta Food Week — 5 dish stamps,
   one special page") give urgency without guilt; a lapsed user loses nothing, ever. **No streaks
   — founder rule, and Duolingo's own leniency data supports it (1.5).** [evidence + founder rule]
4. **Leaderboards: opt-in, celebratory, later.** Strava-style city boards ("most Atlanta icons
   stamped") can motivate, but they cut against no-guilt if ambient. Ship *personal* progress and
   *friend* comparison first (your circle's shared map); public boards only opt-in, only
   positive-framed ("Atlanta's top stampers"), phase 2+. [judgment]
5. **Scene activation, BeReal-style, Atlanta first.** The founder's Atlanta story is already the
   planned first-run explainer; the city has the whale sharks, a walkable icon set, and the
   founder's own network. Concrete v1: a founder-hosted "stamp crawl" (5 icons, one afternoon,
   everyone leaves with a stamped page to post), campus travel/outdoors clubs at GT/Emory/GSU,
   Atlanta food-scene micro-influencers seeded with the food lane's first stamps. Then the two
   national scenes with existing collector identity: **National Parks passport collectors** (clubs
   and blogs already exist — the digital companion pitch) and **study-abroad cohorts** (high
   travel density, BeReal-age). [hypothesis, modeled on evidenced playbooks]

### 4.3 Owning "virtual passport" — SEO/ASO

- The niche is real but tiny and young: Stampie, Stamp, Stamped, PASSPRT, Stamps, CountryLogs —
  App Store listings mostly 2023–2025, country/city-level, several already claiming GPS
  verification (Stamped: "every stamp … through real GPS verification"; PASSPRT: "GPS verified
  stamps"). https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stampie-travel-tracker-map/id6752670353 ·
  https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jc.travelstamp ·
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/passprt-travel-stamps/id6761823922 [evidence]
- **The press hook is live right now:** borders are literally ending physical stamps — Argentina
  2022, Peru 2023, and the EU's Entry/Exit System replacing stamping across 29 countries (rollout
  from late 2025 into 2026; Stampie already markets on this).
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stampie-travel-tracker-map/id6752670353 [evidence]
  Pitch: *"Passport stamps are disappearing. Here's the app bringing them back — with proof."*
  That is our Untappd-Mashable moment; pitch it deliberately (travel press, AJC local angle with
  the Atlanta story).
- **ASO:** keep "Virtual Passport" as the branded phrase everywhere (already the site rule) and
  hold the keyword set: virtual passport, travel stamps, passport stamps, travel stamp app, been
  there tracker. **SEO:** programmatic public pages per stamp/city ("Eiffel Tower stamp," "Atlanta
  travel stamps") from the 18,654-place catalog — competitors physically cannot match the
  catalog's depth; public/private passports (queue item 1) make traveler pages the second
  programmatic surface. [hypothesis, standard programmatic-SEO practice]
- **What "number one in the niche" concretely requires [judgment]:** (1) the loop instrumented
  (installs per shared stamp — the one metric); (2) a catalog + art moat no micro-app can copy
  (18,654 places, scenes, food/wildlife/event lanes); (3) verification *as a ladder*, not a
  gimmick (three proofs — deeper than competitors' single GPS check); (4) the social layer
  (tagging, following, public passports — none of the micro-apps have one); (5) the phrase and
  the press hook claimed this year, before a funded entrant does.

### 4.4 Honesty box — what the evidence does NOT show

- No published study proves stamp-sharing converts to installs at any particular rate; the
  watermark loop is evidenced as a *pattern* (TikTok, Strava, Wordle), not as a guarantee for us.
  Instrument first, celebrate later.
- "Food stamps will make GlobeSkimmers a weekly local habit" is an inference from food-sharing
  evidence, not a measured result — the Atlanta food seed is the cheap experiment that tests it.
- Competitor scan is from store listings, not downloads data; a funded competitor could exist in
  stealth. The founder's queue item 3 already includes a discreet competitor scan — fold this in.
- BeReal's playbook also shows the failure mode: explosive scene-growth, then collapse when the
  artifact stopped being novel. Our hedge is that a passport *accretes* (the collection is worth
  more every year), where a daily photo ritual does not. [hypothesis]
