# LA ecosystem

Research date: 2026-11-26. Method note: the shared WebSearch budget was exhausted before this bucket started, so every finding below comes from fetching the funds' own pages directly (WebFetch). Several sites could not be read (Upfront's and Science's homepages render only a title; upfrontsummit.com has no DNS record; tech-week.com returned 429 twice; F6S and Medium blocked the fetcher; Women Founders Network's homepage returned 404). Those are recorded as Gaps, not guessed. Anything from memory is labelled "possibly outdated" or "unverified".

Fit-note shorthand used below: "the profile" = a solo, first-time, woman founder of Filipino heritage, working full-time in healthcare, LA-based, with a live consumer travel app (iOS + Android) at ~100 users and no revenue, who wants both capital and loud champions.

## Slauson & Co. (and the Friends & Family accelerator)

### Takeaway
The single best thesis match in LA for the profile: an LA pre-seed/seed fund that says it exists to back founders whose "lived experience" is their edge, plus a non-dilutive $20K Friends & Family accelerator that is confirmed running a 2026 cohort. The one thing the site does not show is how to apply.

### Cited Findings
- Self-description: "Early Stage Venture Capital. Investing with Intention. Rooted in Inclusion." and "Slauson exists to back founders of innovative technology companies who use their lived experience as their competitive advantage from the earliest stage." Core belief: "Talent is evenly distributed. Opportunity is not." [Source](https://www.slauson.co/)
- Stage: Pre-seed and Seed. Geography: US-based; "a Los Angeles based early-stage venture capital firm." No founder-location requirement stated. Check size not stated. [Source](https://www.slauson.co/)
- Team listed: Austin Clements (Managing Partner, co-founder), Ajay Relan (Managing Partner), Brittany Crockett (Platform Partner), Miki Reynolds (Investor), Waverly Middelton (Senior Associate), Clarissa Kim (Content Director). [Source](https://www.slauson.co/)
- Fund II announcement shown on the homepage, linking a TechCrunch article (September 2024) reporting a $100M Fund II, up from a $75M Fund I. No 2025–2026 fund mentioned. [Source](https://www.slauson.co/)
- Friends & Family: "20 companies across the country will receive $20,000 in non-dilutive capital" in a 12-week accelerator (announcement undated on the page). [Source](https://www.slauson.co/)
- Friends & Family page is headed "Friends & Family 2026 Cohort", with a "MEET THE COMPANIES" link to a Substack post; alumni pages exist for Winter 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022. Purpose: "An extension of Slauson & Co.'s mission of economic inclusion"; it helps founders "strengthen their business, attract investment, and build a network with other founders living a similar experience." Selection statement: "We identify founders uniquely positioned to build something new." [Source](https://www.slauson.co/friends-and-family)
- No application form, pitch email, or "we read every…" statement on the homepage; the only call to action is a newsletter sign-up. [Source](https://www.slauson.co/)
- Portfolio consumer examples listed: ABBY (AI therapy app), El Camino ("travel booking platform for single, female travelers"), Livin, Cohart, Pressed Roots, Sienna Naturals, Tumoni (US–Central America remittances), allUp, Flora, Zenblen, Misha's, Slate. [Source](https://www.slauson.co/)
- Media/community pull: Inclusion Reports for 2021–2024 published; press from TechCrunch (Fund II), Forbes, and NYT (PayPal's investment in Black- and Latino-led VC firms); Instagram, LinkedIn, newsletter. No events listed. [Source](https://www.slauson.co/)

### Inferences
- A travel app for an under-served traveller segment (El Camino) is already in the portfolio, so a consumer travel thesis is not foreign to them; it also means a possible portfolio-overlap question.
- Alumni pages labelled "Winter 2025" suggest the accelerator runs on a winter cycle; a "2026 Cohort" page existing in November 2026 suggests the 2026 class is already selected and applications for the next class would likely open before or around year-end (unverified).
- Non-dilutive $20K plus the firm's press reach makes F&F the highest champion-value, lowest-cost ask in the bucket for the profile. The "across the country" wording means no LA advantage, but being LA-based does not hurt.
- Miki Reynolds (listed as Investor) co-founded Grid110 (memory, unverified here): if true, the Grid110 → Slauson pipeline is a real path.

### Gaps
- No application link, deadline, or cohort dates for Friends & Family found on slauson.co; ff.slauson.co redirects back to the same page. The "MEET THE COMPANIES" Substack URL was not captured (slauson.substack.com is an unrelated publication).
- Check size for direct fund investments, solo-founder stance, and whether F&F is in-person (LA) or virtual: not stated.
- Fund II date is as cited on the homepage's TechCrunch link (September 2024), not independently fetched.

## Mucker Capital / MuckerLab

### Takeaway
One of the few LA programs that will look at a pre-product company and accepts applications from anywhere, but it wants "100% committed" founders and says consumer companies "need more proof of product-market fit". Terms are $100–175K for 10–15%. Realistic after traction, not now.

### Cited Findings
- "MuckerLab typically invests between $100,000 to $175,000 in exchange for a 10% to 15% equity stake." Average company "works with us for roughly 12 months" (range 3–24 months); "No three-month bootcamps. No demo days." [Source](https://www.mucker.com/muckerlab)
- Applications open, "reviewed quarterly"; annual cohorts of 20–25 companies; info-session webinar offered. Application asks for deck link, product stage, ARR, MoM growth, capital raised/sought. [Source](https://www.mucker.com/muckerlab)
- Selection: "We look for passionate, 100% committed entrepreneurs of any age and experience level with disruptive ideas", with "a strong preference for fully formed teams of builders". Pre-product accepted; "Enterprise and B2B startups can be earlier-stage. Consumer companies need more proof of product-market fit." [Source](https://www.mucker.com/muckerlab)
- Categories include Consumer FinTech, DTC, Media, Social Media, Other. Applications accepted worldwide, no relocation; offices in Los Angeles, Austin, Toronto. Rejected companies encouraged to reapply "as traction improves". [Source](https://www.mucker.com/muckerlab)
- Mucker (fund) describes "Seed and Series A funding", invests "across the United States, Canada, and beyond"; exits cited: Honey (acquired by PayPal for $4B), ServiceTitan (IPO). MuckerLab "limits its cohorts to 20-25 companies per year". [Source](https://ai.knwn.app/mucker//)
- mucker.com now redirects to an AI-chat site (mucker.knwnapp.com → ai.knwn.app/mucker); /apply returned 404; no partner names were readable. Partners from memory: Erik Rannala, William Hsu — possibly outdated/unverified.

### Inferences
- A founder with a full-time job runs straight into "100% committed"; a consumer app at ~100 users runs into "more proof of product-market fit". Apply only once either changes (e.g., clear retention/growth curve), and attend the free info session first — it's the cheapest way to get read.
- 10–15% for ~$150K prices the company at roughly $1–1.5M post; compare before accepting.

### Gaps
- Direct application URL, latest fund size/vintage, decision timeline beyond "quarterly", any solo-founder or diversity statements: not found.

## Tech Coast Angels (TCA Venture Group) and Pasadena Angels

### Takeaway
TCA is the big SoCal angel network (≈400 members, no application fee at the LA chapter, funds invest $100K+) but wants "some level of market validation or traction" and "ideally" revenue; Pasadena Angels (a TCA network member) says solo founders are "typically not considered" and it "rarely" invests in consumer products. Neither is a now-target at ~100 users.

### Cited Findings (TCA)
- "Approximately 400 members"; "six regional networks covering Orange County, Los Angeles, Pasadena, Inland Empire, Florida/Georgia"; investments in 540+ companies since 1997, $300M+ deployed; stage "seed, bridge, and early A-round"; "prioritize[s] supporting California-based entrepreneurs". [Source](https://tcaventuregroup.com/)
- Application via SSD SPV Hub for TCA LA, Pasadena Angels and Inland Empire (e.g., tcala.ssdspvhub.com/register-company); TCA OC has its own application reached after a Stripe payment and "will be charging a nominal admin application fee", while the general guidance says "We don't charge entrepreneurs an application fee". Process: web application → staff/industry panel review → 8–15 minute pre-screen → 15+15 minute screening (3–4 companies per bi-monthly session) → due diligence → optional monthly meeting → funding. Of 1,000+ companies reviewed a year, ~25% reach screening and ~25% of those reach diligence. Members typically invest ≥$25K each; TCA funds typically ≥$100K per company; 30–40 new ventures funded a year. Traction: "Some level of market validation or traction"; revenue "ideally". Sectors include "Consumer Products and Services"; TCA LA focuses on technology, adtech, healthtech, media, entertainment, SaaS. Return case "10x or better". Geography "Mostly US based, CA preference"; roughly half of investments in Southern California. [Source](https://tcaventuregroup.com/apply-for-funding-now/)

### Cited Findings (Pasadena Angels)
- "close to 100 investor members"; "over $75 million in 250+ startup funding rounds"; PA Fund III, a $1.5M fund formed in 2024 that co-invests only alongside members; founded 2000; nonprofit 501(c)6; members invest individually. [Source](https://pasadenaangels.com/)
- Apply at pasadena.ssdspvhub.com/register-company; "typically respond within 1-3 weeks"; monthly screening (last Wednesday) and breakfast pitch (first Wednesday); typical investment $150K–$750K; valuation caps usually $1M–$15M, preferring single digits; stage "Seed+" with at least an MVP and completed customer discovery; focus on technology, especially SaaS; "rarely invest in consumer products or real estate"; requires a US C-corp; "Two or more founders. Solo founders are typically not considered."; potential to become a "$20M+ company within 3-5 years". [Source](https://www.pasadenaangels.com/entrepreneurs)

### Inferences
- Pasadena Angels is effectively a no for a solo consumer founder. TCA LA is a "later" — once there is validation worth 15 minutes of screening. Neither publicises founders much; champion value is low.

### Gaps
- TCA decision timeline, 2024–2026 deal counts, any women/underrepresented statements (none on the pages fetched).
