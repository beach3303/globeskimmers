# AAPI and Filipino-focused capital

Research date: 2026-11-26. **Method caveat:** the shared web-search budget for this turn was already exhausted when this bucket started, so every finding below comes from direct reads of the organizations' own websites (plus their linked program/apply pages). No press cross-checks (TechCrunch, Axios, Tech in Asia, e27, Philippine press) were possible; anything the fund's own site does not state is marked **unverified**, and anything carried over from pre-2025 knowledge is marked **possibly outdated**. Four Philippine sites could not be reached at all today (Foxmont, Gentree, Core Capital, MAIN) — see their sections.

Profile used for fit notes: solo, first-time, woman founder of Filipino heritage, US citizen, US-incorporated, full-time healthcare professional in Los Angeles, live consumer travel app (iOS + Android) built alone with AI tools in eight months, ~100 users, no revenue; wants money and loud champions.

## Hyphen Capital

### Takeaway
The single best mandate fit in this bucket: an active (essays dated Nov 2026) pre-seed-through-growth fund that exists to back Asian American founders, lists "consumer" as a sector, takes cold inbound through a form it says it reads in full, and does not require a lead. The floor check ($150K) means she would need to be raising an actual round, not a $25K angel ticket.

### Cited Findings
- Founder and Managing Partner is Dave Lu (ex-Yahoo!, Apple, eBay, Cisco; founded Fanpop and Expo; started the Asian American Founders Circle in 2011, "400+ founders today"; produced the Emmy-winning documentary *38 at the Garden*). No other partners named. [Source](https://www.hyphencap.com/)
- Thesis: Hyphen "bets on founders based on what they can build, not who they know"; targets "Asian American entrepreneurs who were raised to play it safe." Sectors: "software, AI infra, consumer, and beyond." [Source](https://www.hyphencap.com/)
- Stage: pre-seed through growth, "stage agnostic." Check: "anywhere from $150,000 to $5,000,000 depending on the size and stage of the deal." "High conviction, no lead required." [Source](https://www.hyphencap.com/)
- Track record stated: "$35M+ invested, 100+ companies backed"; network of "1,000+ founders and operators," roster of 85+ members. Portfolio names: Persona, Gamma, Super.com, Formation Bio, Nitra, Slope, Fish Audio, Mila, Nex, Ploy, DensityAI, Simplify; exits SafeBase (Drata), Pry (Brex), Revela (Oddity). [Source](https://www.hyphencap.com/)
- Activity evidence: essays on the site dated Sep 3, Nov 4 and Nov 16, 2026. [Source](https://www.hyphencap.com/)
- Pitch route is a form ("Pitch Hyphen"): name, founder/investor, LinkedIn, email, company name, brief description (what it does, who it serves, problem), optional deck link. Form text: the firm "invests in exceptional Asian American founders"; "stage agnostic," founders "often meet it early, including around pre-seed"; "the team reads every submission and contacts people when there is a potential fit." No response-time commitment, no email address given. [Source](https://hyphencap.com/contact)
- The /thesis page is a May 2020 Dave Lu essay (**possibly outdated**); it describes Hyphen as an "early-stage venture firm ... that invests in Asian American founders" and points founders to "complete our intake form." It name-checks Gold House, the Asian Hustle Network and the Asian American Founders Circle as community efforts. [Source](https://hyphencap.com/thesis)

### Inferences
- "Asian American" as used here plainly includes Filipino-American; nothing on the site narrows it to East/South Asian founders.
- No stated US-incorporation or geography rule, but every visible signal (SF Lunar New Year banquet, Silicon Valley roots) is US-centric — a Delaware/US company in LA is squarely inside the comfort zone.
- Amplification is real but personality-driven: Dave Lu writes regularly (three essays in autumn 2026), runs a 1,000+ founder network and has a mainstream media footprint (Emmy documentary). Expect champion value if he says yes; the fund does not appear to run a PR machine for portfolio companies.
- The $150K minimum is the hard part. Realistic path: pitch now for feedback/network, be explicit that the round is small, and ask whether Hyphen does smaller first checks or scout/angel allocations.

### Gaps
- Fund size, latest fund vintage, number of women / solo / first-time founders backed — not on the site; **unverified**.
- "Model Majority" events: not mentioned anywhere on the current site; **unverified** whether Hyphen still runs them.
- Whether any portfolio company is a consumer travel or consumer-social mobile app — the site does not describe products; **unverified**.
- Decision speed: not stated.

## Gold House Ventures / Gold House Ventures Accelerator (Founder Fellowship) / Gold Rush

### Takeaway
Mandate fit is excellent (built for Asian Pacific founders, now "all underrepresented communities nationwide," 133 AP-founded companies supported) and Gold House is the loudest possible champion in the AAPI world, but the accelerator's last listed cohort is Spring 2025, no 2026/2027 cohort or deadline is posted, and the Gold Rush application URL returns 404 — current program status is **unverified**. The live door is the fund's email inbox.

### Cited Findings
- Gold House Ventures: "the definitive early stage fund investing in industry-shifting startups led by best-in-class multicultural founders," an "affinity-based, returns-driven model" backing "next-generation Asian Pacific operators." Stage: early stage. Check size and equity terms not stated. Founders are told to share what they are building at **investments@goldhouse.org**. The investor "Venture Network" is invite-only; LPs join automatically. Footer "© 2026"; nominations for the 2027 Gold100 are open. [Source](https://goldhouse.org/ventures)
- Accelerator / Founder Fellowship: a 14-week program "for diverse-led tech startups across the United States"; began in 2020 for Asian Pacific founders and "expanded to all underrepresented communities nationwide." Since 2020: 133 Asian Pacific-founded companies that have raised more than $2B in follow-on capital. Benefits: weekly masterclasses (brand equity, product-market fit, human capital), 1:1 advising, curated investor introductions, public Demo Day, Panda and Din Tai Fung perks, co-working; founders are "Eligble for seed funding" (sic) — no amount or terms. Cohorts listed Spring 2020 through **Spring 2025**; no 2026/2027 dates; no apply button on the page. Advisors listed from Coatue, a16z crypto, Golden Gate Ventures, Zeal Capital Partners, Gusto. Alumni named: Airboxr, Book An Author, Brick, CircleHealth, Crossingz, Dart Technologies, Giftpack, Imera, Khoobi, LegalEase, Owle AI, Raycaster (YC F24), RURBAN, Root Access, Sphere, Sugarwork, Tarragon Systems, Turmerik. Consumer-facing examples: Khoobi (beauty-tech), Imera (immigrant services). [Source](https://goldhouse.org/founderfellowship/) [Source](https://goldhouse.org/futures-entrepreneurship/)
- Gold Rush: described as a "cohort of Founders" to "accelerate your business"; seasons listed from Fall 2019 through **Spring 2022** only (**possibly outdated**); apply links point to goldhouse.org/gold-rush-application/ and goldhouse.org/goldrush/apply/; no eligibility, benefit or deadline text. [Source](https://goldhouse.org/goldrush/apply/)
- The Gold Rush application page (goldhouse.org/gold-rush-application/) returned **HTTP 404** on 2026-11-26.

### Inferences
- The accelerator looks paused or between cycles; Spring 2025 is the newest cohort shown and the site has no 2026 call. Treat "Gold House Futures accelerator 2026/2027 cohort" as **unverified** until Gold House posts one.
- A cold email to investments@goldhouse.org is the only live channel; the fund's own language ("industry-shifting," "best-in-class") and the alumni list (mostly B2B/AI) suggest they expect more traction than ~100 users.
- Amplification power is the highest in this bucket (Gold100/A100 lists, gala, mainstream press). That matters even for a non-investment relationship: Gold House runs honors, not just a fund.

- Count of women / solo founders in portfolio — not stated.

## Kapor Capital

### Takeaway
Open, 5-minute cold application with a clearly documented ~two-week call sequence, and 80% of Fund III founders are underrepresented POC and/or women — but the thesis is strict: the company must close an access/opportunity/outcome gap for low-income communities and/or communities of color in the US. A consumer travel-passport app only fits if that gap is real and front-and-center, which it is not today.

### Cited Findings
- Thesis: "We invest in early stage startups that close gaps of access for all"; sectors education, work, finance, justice, food, health. $106M invested in total; 180+ portfolio companies; Fund III: 80% of founders identify as underrepresented people of color and/or women. No partners named on the homepage. © 2026. [Source](https://www.kaporcapital.com/)
- Consumer examples in portfolio: Thrive Market, Kai XR (kids' smartphone experiences), Newsela, Promise. [Source](https://www.kaporcapital.com/)
- Pitch page: an online application "should only take 5 minutes to complete" (Basic Details → Open Response → Upload Pitch). Pre-criteria: tech-driven, early stage; closes gaps in access to information or goods/services, expands economic opportunity, or improves market-based solutions; founders commit to "building a diverse team and inclusive company culture." Oakland, CA office. [Source](https://www.kaporcapital.com/pitch)
- Investment criteria: pre-seed, seed, Series A entry points; companies "that close a significant access, opportunity, or outcome gap for low-income communities and/or communities of color in the U.S."; not a fit if past Series A, if product not built/deployed, or if "no direct impact on underrepresented communities." Process: team review → ~two weeks of calls (30-min intro, 45–60-min demo/Q&A, partner meeting) → diligence → two managing partners decide by consensus. Founders' Commitment ("G.I.V.E.") launched January 2016 (**possibly outdated** as a detail, still displayed). [Source](https://www.kaporcapital.com/investment-criteria)

### Inferences
- A solo founder with a built, deployed product clears the "product deployed" bar; the US-focus and diverse-founder stats are favorable. The gap-closing test is the disqualifier unless the app is framed around access (e.g., diaspora families, first-generation travelers) with evidence.
- Fast, transparent cold process — low cost to try, low odds.

### Gaps
- Check size, latest fund (Fund IV?) size/year, current partner names — not on the pages read; **unverified**.
- No 2025–2026 dated deals visible; activity level **unverified**.

Items dated: "Pitch to Founders 2025," "Founder-Overview-Nov-2026," uploads Oct 2025 and Feb 2026; © 2026. [Source](https://harlem.capital/)

[Source](https://harlem.capital/pitch/)
