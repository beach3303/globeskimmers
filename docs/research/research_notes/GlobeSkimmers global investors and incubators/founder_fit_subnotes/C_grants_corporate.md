# Equity-free grants, fellowships and corporate programs

Research date: 2026-11-26. Method note: the shared web-search budget for this turn was already exhausted when this bucket started, so every fact below comes from direct fetches of the programs' own pages (no search engine, no archive/proxy). Pages that blocked or did not render are named in each program's Gaps. Anything recalled from before 2025 and not re-confirmed is marked "possibly outdated"; anything not confirmed from a page this session is marked "unverified".

Profile used for fit notes: solo, first-time woman founder; full-time clinician whose venture is a consumer travel app, not a healthcare product; Los Angeles; iOS + Android app live; ~100 users; no revenue; wants cash and public champions.

## Tory Burch Foundation Fellows Program

### Takeaway
Not eligible today: the program requires a minimum of $75,000 in annual revenue. The 2026-27 window closed on November 17, 2026 (nine days before this note); fellows are chosen in May 2027. Revisit the moment revenue clears $75k, because it is one of the loudest women-founder platforms in the US.

### Cited Findings
- Applications for the current cycle opened September 22, 2026 and closed November 17, 2026; review runs Winter 2026-27; "Fellows selection: May 2027" [Source](https://www.toryburchfoundation.org/fellows/)
- Applicant must be "a woman entrepreneur who owns the largest or equally largest stake in a qualifying business," have a significant management role, be 21 or older as of November 17, 2026, be proficient in English, and be a legal U.S. resident (territories included) [Source](https://www.toryburchfoundation.org/fellows/)
- Company must be for-profit (any industry), "majority (51% or more) owned and controlled by women," formed under U.S. law and operating in the U.S., and must generate "a minimum of $75,000 annually, with no maximum revenue requirement" [Source](https://www.toryburchfoundation.org/fellows/)
- What fellows get is framed as community, advisors, support (coaching, guidance, resources) and recognition; the page does not state a grant dollar amount or cohort size [Source](https://www.toryburchfoundation.org/fellows/)
- A fellow testimonial on the page mentions "a small interest-free loan," presented as a one-off, not a standard benefit [Source](https://www.toryburchfoundation.org/fellows/)

### Inferences
- The next window should open around September 2027 for a 2027-28 cohort if the cadence holds (inference from the 2026 dates, not stated by the Foundation).
- Earlier cycles were publicised as 50 fellows per year with a $5,000 education grant and a Fellows Week in New York (possibly outdated; from memory, not on the 2026 page).
- Media value is high: the Foundation and Tory Burch's own channels name every fellow, and the cohort announcement is routinely covered by business press (possibly outdated impression; not re-verified this session).

### Gaps
- Grant amount, cohort size, years-in-business floor, and the Fellowship Rules PDF were not retrievable (the /programs/fellows/, /fellows/faq/, /fellows-faq/ and /faq/ paths all 404). Unverified.
- Whether a company under $75k revenue can apply "aspirationally" is not addressed; the wording reads as a hard floor.

## Cartier Women's Initiative

### Takeaway
Not eligible now: regional awards require $50k–$5M revenue in the last closed fiscal year, 5–250 team members, and alignment with a UN SDG. The 2027 edition closed June 16, 2026. It is the single most prestigious, most heavily publicised program in this bucket, so it belongs on a 2028-edition watch list rather than the 2027 to-do list.

### Cited Findings
- Regional award prizes: first place $100,000, second $60,000, third $30,000 (grants) [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/regional-awards)
- Nine regions including North America; three fellows per region per edition plus three for the thematic Science & Technology Pioneer Award [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/awards)
- Regional-award eligibility: "generated annual revenue between $50,000 USD and $5 million USD in its last closed fiscal year," 5 to 250 team members (full-time, part-time, seasonal or contractors), no more than $2 million raised in equity or quasi-equity, for-profit, a woman in a main leadership role, 18+ [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/regional-awards)
- "We do not accept applications from non-profit entities"; a woman "must fill one of the main leadership positions" and "must own a share of the business that is greater or equal to that of her co-founder(s)"; the business "must align with at least one of the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)"; one business and one award category per edition [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/faq)
- Science & Technology Pioneer Award: at least TRL4, commercialising for no more than 5 years, no Series A raised [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/faq)
- Timing for the edition whose deadline was "June 16, 2026, 2PM (CEST / GMT+2)": applicants notified "by December 2026," fellows announced spring 2027 [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/faq)
- "Applications are now closed for the 2027 edition" [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/regional-awards)
- 2026 fellows announced March 26, 2026; awards ceremony Bangkok, June 10, 2026 [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/)
- Fellowship benefits: leadership communications and media training, the in-person INSEAD Women's Impact Entrepreneurship Program, 1:1 executive coaching by The Women's Impact Alliance, "international exposure and media visibility," jury mentorship, a 500+ member lifelong community [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/regional-awards)
- Programme-year rhythm: Jan–May media training and visual assets; June induction, INSEAD, Awards Week and Ceremony; Jul–Dec customised training and coaching [Source](https://www.cartierwomensinitiative.com/fellowship-timeline)

### Inferences
- If the cadence holds, the 2028 edition will open in roughly spring 2027 with a deadline around June 2027 (inference; the site does not publish 2028 dates yet).
- A pre-revenue consumer travel app fails three gates at once (revenue floor, team size, SDG impact framing). Even with revenue, the "women impact entrepreneurs" positioning means the app would need a credible SDG story.

### Gaps
- Application-opening date for the 2028 edition: not published.
- Whether the 2027 Terms & Conditions changed anything (file named "2027_-_CWI_Terms_and_Conditions" is linked from the FAQ but was not read).

## Amber Grant (WomensNet)

### Takeaway
The best immediate fit in this bucket: explicitly open to idea-stage and pre-revenue women-owned businesses in the US or Canada, a $15 fee, a $15,000 monthly Startup Grant for businesses under $10,000 in sales, and every monthly winner is automatically in the running for three $75,000 year-end grants. Publicity is modest (WomensNet site, Facebook, email list, winner videos), not national press.

### Cited Findings
- "Three $15,000 Amber Grants each month": the Monthly Amber Grant, the Monthly Startup Grant, and a Business Category Grant (one category per calendar month) [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/)
- Startup Grant: $15,000 monthly "for businesses in the idea phase or with minimal sales (under $10,000)" [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/)
- Year-end: $75,000 grants to three winners, drawn from monthly winners; announced in January [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/) and [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/faq/)
- Eligibility: woman aged 18+, business at least 50% women-owned, based in the United States or Canada; idea-stage and pre-revenue businesses can apply [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/faq/)
- Fee: "a nominal $15 application fee," "the only fee we charge"; waiver available by emailing info@ambergrantsforwomen.com [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/faq/)
- One application covers all WomensNet grants; apply at ambergrantsforwomen.com/get-an-amber-grant/apply-now/ [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/)
- Deadline: 11:59:59 pm Eastern on the last day of each month; applications do not roll forward to the next month; the system blocks a second application from the same email within 3 months and WomensNet advises reapplying only after a significant positive change [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/faq/)
- Category-grant applications stay active for 12 months without reapplying [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/)
- Winners announced by the 21st of the following month via Facebook, the website and the email list; winners contacted from an @ambergrantsforwomen.com address [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/faq/)
- Winner profiles with videos and a "Past Recipients" section are published on the site [Source](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/)

### Inferences
- Applying by November 30, 2026 enters the November cycle; a Startup Grant application is the natural lane (sales under $10,000). Because of the 3-month block, time the application to follow a visible milestone (store build, user count, press).
- Amplification is community-scale: a WomensNet winner video and listing, useful as third-party validation in later applications, not a press event.

### Gaps
- Selection criteria beyond "strength of application" are not published. Whether an unincorporated sole proprietor qualifies as a "business" is not stated (the FAQ only requires women ownership and a US/Canada base).

## AWS Activate

### Takeaway
Activate Founders is open to bootstrapped founders: $1,000 to start, up to $5,000 for selected participants, 5–10 business-day decisions. Activate Portfolio (up to $200,000) requires an Org ID from an accelerator, angel or VC, so it is out of reach until she joins one. Like Azure credits, value depends on actually running something on AWS.

### Cited Findings
- Activate Founders: "Apply directly if you're bootstrapped or self-funded"; participants start with $1,000 and "Select participants may qualify for additional credits up to $5,000" [Source](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits)
- Activate Portfolio: up to $200,000 USD; "You must have an Organization ID (Org ID) from your Activate Provider" (accelerator, angel investor or VC); pre-Series B, founded within the last 10 years, on a paid AWS account plan [Source](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits)
- AWS Credits for AI Startups: invite-only after Portfolio, $200,000+ scaled to workload [Source](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits)
- Kiro Startup Credits listed for startups without current Activate Credits [Source](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits)
- Process: AWS Builder ID with a professional email, choose tier, describe product/funding stage/company, link an AWS account, response in 5–10 business days [Source](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits)

### Inferences
- Any accelerator she later joins (e.g., the YC/Speedrun-type programs in the other buckets) typically is an Activate Provider, which is how founders reach the $100k–$200k Portfolio tier.

### Gaps
- Credit validity period and 2025–2026 changes: not stated on the page.

## Verizon Small Business Digital Ready ($10,000 grants)

### Takeaway
Eligible and open now: any US-based for-profit small business owner 18+ who completes two free Digital Ready courses or events in 2026 can submit one application; ten $10,000 grants are awarded each month June–December 2026, with final decisions by January 12, 2027. No revenue or years-in-business floor is stated. Selection starts with an anonymous drawing weighted toward under-resourced communities, so odds are lottery-like and the publicity is minimal.

### Cited Findings
- "Applications for our 2026 $10,000 grants are now open"; "$15M in small business grants have been awarded" since 2021 [Source](https://digitalready.verizonwireless.com/)
- Eligibility: for-profit small businesses based in the U.S., Puerto Rico or the U.S. Virgin Islands; owner 18+; owners of multiple businesses may apply for one; "Register and complete any combination of two free courses or events anytime in 2026"; reviewed monthly June–December 2026; one application stays eligible for the rest of the year; final decisions by January 12, 2027; 10 businesses selected each month [Source](https://digitalready.verizonwireless.com/funding)
- Details: nonprofits not eligible; no revenue, employee or years-in-business limits stated; course-completion window given as January 1 to December 7, 2026 in the FAQ; LISC is the independent grant administrator (intake, review, distribution); selection by anonymous drawing, an algorithm balancing industries and geographies if needed, priority for businesses in under-resourced low-to-moderate-income communities and for first-time recipients, in-depth review of a subset, then verification of financial and legal standing [Source](https://digitalready.verizonwireless.com/funding/details)
- Platform: 70+ free online courses (financial management, social media marketing, AI adoption), free coaching, community [Source](https://www.verizon.com/about/responsibility/digital-inclusion/small-business-training)

### Inferences

Finalists' "financial and legal standing" verification suggests a registered business and bank account will be required at award time.
- A 2027 program is likely but not announced; the courses are cheap to complete and would carry over as a credential.

### Gaps

## NASE Growth Grants

### Takeaway
Up to $4,000 for NASE members, reviewed quarterly; a pre-revenue business may apply with projected income and expense statements (the page allows projections for new businesses but does not explicitly confirm pre-revenue eligibility). Requires a paid membership ($120/year Annual, immediate eligibility; Monthly $11.95 becomes eligible after 90 days). No publicity to speak of.

### Cited Findings
- "Up to $4,000"; NASE may award less or more than requested or none; must be a member in good standing through the award date; Annual, Veteran and Gold members immediately eligible, Monthly members after 90 days; other levels not eligible [Source](https://www.nase.org/become-a-member/grants-and-scholarships/growth-grants)
- Permitted uses include marketing and advertising, part-time hires, equipment and software, a new website, trade shows, training; not debt or rent [Source](https://www.nase.org/become-a-member/grants-and-scholarships/growth-grants)
- Required documents: grant-use statement, business plan, recent tax return or P&L (new businesses may submit expected P&L or income/expense projections), a photo; applications accepted anytime via the members portal and reviewed quarterly (Oct–Dec submissions reviewed in January); notification by email within 30 days; funds by check within 30 days of paperwork [Source](https://www.nase.org/become-a-member/grants-and-scholarships/growth-grants)
- Membership prices: Annual $120/year, Monthly $11.95/month, Gold $45/month, Veteran $99/year, Student $25/year [Source](https://www.nase.org/become-a-member/membership-options)

### Inferences
- Net expected value is small ($4,000 cap against a $120 membership and a business plan), but it is one of very few grants that explicitly accommodates businesses without a tax return.

### Gaps
- Explicit pre-revenue eligibility and selection odds: not published.
