# Women-founder VC funds and angel networks

Research date: 2026-11-26. Method note: the shared web-search budget for this run was exhausted before any search ran, so every finding below comes from fetching each fund's own site (plus two press pages those sites link to). That favours primary sources but means third-party confirmation (fund closes, departures, litigation) was mostly unavailable; such items are flagged "unverified" or "from memory, possibly outdated". Fit notes refer to "the founder" = solo, first-time, Filipino-American woman, full-time healthcare professional in Los Angeles, live iOS+Android consumer travel app, ~100 users, no revenue.

## Female Founders Fund (NYC)

### Takeaway
Still investing and the loudest press machine in this bucket (Fortune covers it several times a year), but it is a seed fund writing $500K–$750K into 6–8 companies a year and, as of October 2026, tilting 10–20% of its fund toward AI deals. A pre-revenue consumer app with ~100 users is early for them; the open Typeform is worth a shot mainly to start the relationship they say they like to build "months before" a raise.

### Cited Findings
- Team: Anu Duggal (Founding Partner), Julie Cerick (Head of Platform), Zabreen Khan (Principal), Layla Alexander (Associate), Shirley Wang (Platform Operations Associate). [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/)
- Thesis: "We invest in women building category defining businesses." Stated focus areas: digital healthcare, AI-first vertical software, beauty & personal care, pet, deep tech. [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/)
- Stage and check: "As seed investors, we invest primarily at the seed-stage (both pre-launch and post-launch)"; check size $500K–$750K; about 6–8 new investments per year; they lead, co-lead or participate. [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/resources/)
- Eligibility: "We invest in companies that have at least one female founder as part of the founding team." Geography: NYC-based but "geographically agnostic", with past investments in Los Angeles, Santa Monica, SF and London. [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/resources/)
- Process: first meeting → internal review → diligence (several weeks) → references; "from first meeting to closing a round, our timeline is roughly 4-6 weeks." No warm-intro requirement stated; no stated traction threshold ("We invest first and foremost in people"). [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/resources/)
- How to pitch: Typeform https://femalefoundersfund.typeform.com/pitches (the FAQ also lists an older fcubed.typeform.com/pitches); general email hello@femalefoundersfund.com. [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/)
- Strategy change (Fortune, 2026-10-07): the firm's "$29 million fund" typically targeted 8–10% ownership at seed; it is now allocating 10–20% of the fund to AI companies without that ownership target, and published a map of 100+ female AI founders; the report notes consumer AI gets the least capital. Quote: "We decided to come up with a new strategy." [Source](https://fortune.com/2026/10/07/female-founders-fund-ai-complete-list-fidji-simo-mira-murati/)
- Fund history on their press page: $57M third fund (2021) is the most recent fund-size figure shown there — possibly outdated relative to the $29M figure Fortune cites. [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/press/)
- Consumer evidence 2025–2026 (portfolio exits/news): Zola to be acquired by The Knot Worldwide (Reuters, 2026-10-14); Co-Star acquired by Midjourney (Bloomberg, 2026-07-24); Fable joined Everand/Scribd (2025-06-09); 831 Stories (NPR, 2025-09-25). [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/press/)
- Media/community pull: Fortune features on the fund (2026-03-24, 2026-06-23 "second-time female founders are on the rise", 2026-10-07); podcast "The Two Percent"; annual Camp FFF retreat (campfff.com); blog; events page. [Source](https://femalefoundersfund.com/)

### Inferences
- The 2026 pieces (AI landscape, second-time founders) suggest the current sweet spot is repeat founders and AI; a first-time, pre-revenue consumer founder is off the centre of that beam.
- Their "pre-launch" seed language and "relationship months before a raise" language mean a polite Typeform submission now, with a product demo, costs nothing and builds a file.
- Amplification is the highest in this bucket: an FFF cheque reliably produces a Fortune mention.

### Gaps
- Current fund number and vintage (is the $29M the fourth fund?) — unverified.
- Whether cold Typeform submissions are read/answered — not stated.
- Any Filipino/AAPI or solo-founder examples — not found on fetched pages.

## BBG Ventures (NYC)

### Takeaway
Active pre-seed/seed fund that leads rounds with $500K–$2M and has the most consumer-app-heavy portfolio here (including a travel-itinerary app). Mission has broadened from women to "founders with lived experience." The open Typeform fits mechanically, but a lead cheque of $500K+ implies a priced pre-seed round the founder is not yet ready to run.

### Cited Findings
- Partners featured: Susan Lyne and Nisha Dua. [Source](https://www.bbgventures.com/)
- Stage pre-seed and seed; "Check size: $500k–$2M"; they "lead or help shape rounds, often take board seats"; "Our goal is to be on your team." [Source](https://www.bbgventures.com/about-us)
- Who they back: "Early-stage investors in founders with lived experience." "We look for founders whose diverse background or lived experience gives them a unique competitive advantage." BBG "began by backing female founders in the mobile era and has since broadened its lens to diverse teams." [Source](https://www.bbgventures.com/about-us)
- Thesis frames: "Deep Technology Leverage" (AI that drives new behaviour/economics) and "Deep Humanity" (social/emotional/physical needs AI can't close); sectors health, work, vertical AI, consumer, fintech, climate; they avoid the "Commodity Middle" (thin wrappers). [Source](https://www.bbgventures.com/)
- How to pitch: Typeform https://947689.typeform.com/to/FPR7znn6 or hello@bbgventures.com; no cold-inbound policy stated. [Source](https://www.bbgventures.com/)
- Portfolio: 52 companies listed; consumer apps include Mozi (private social network), Scout (shopping assistant), Winnie (childcare marketplace), Fiveable, a co-watching app; travel: boopwithme.com ("turns real trips into shareable, shoppable itineraries") and Naya Homes (short-term rentals, LatAm). No investment dates shown. [Source](https://www.bbgventures.com/companies)
- Community pull: Substack newsletter https://bbgventures.substack.com/, blog; proprietary research "Investing in the Polycultural Future of America" (2,000 Americans surveyed). [Source](https://www.bbgventures.com/)

### Inferences
- The boopwithme investment is a double-edged signal: BBGV understands travel-sharing consumer apps, but may see a passport/stamps app as adjacent to an existing bet.
- "Lived experience" framing fits a healthcare-professional immigrant-heritage founder building for travellers like herself; the pitch should lean on that.
- Fund size/vintage is not on the site (from memory Fund III was ~$50M in 2021 — possibly outdated).

### Gaps
- Latest fund size and year — not stated.
- Whether they consider solo founders or pre-revenue apps — not stated.
- Decision speed — not stated.

## Halogen Ventures (Los Angeles)

### Takeaway
The one LA-based, woman-led, consumer-friendly fund in this bucket, actively deploying a $30M Fund III (closed June 2025) and very visible in press. The catch: Fund III's stated thesis is "the future of family" (childcare etc.), and pitches go by plain email with no stated process. Worth a tight, LA-flavoured cold email; low odds unless the app is framed around family travel.

### Cited Findings
- Jesse Draper, Founding Partner, "fourth-generation venture capitalist and one of the first solo female GPs in Los Angeles"; based in Los Angeles. [Source](https://www.halogenvc.com/)
- Thesis: early-stage consumer technology and B2B, "focused on family, consumer innovation, and emerging tech verticals"; "widely recognized for backing female founders and mixed-gender teams"; stats 100+ investments, six unicorns, $15B+ market value, $4B+ follow-on raised. [Source](https://www.halogenvc.com/)
- Fund III: closed a $30M Fund III on 2025-06-27 "focused on the future of family, including childcare" (TechCrunch, Fortune, AP coverage listed); earlier fund $21M (Feb 2021). [Source](https://www.halogenvc.com/latest-news)
- 2025–2026 activity: first nine Alabama investments announced 2026-03-05; "another $1M in six Alabama startups" (Birmingham Business Journal, 2026-03-04); partnership with Innovate Alabama to invest $10M in female founders; milestone "Over 100 Female-Founded Investments"; Jesse Draper on Inc.'s 2025 Female Founders 500; LA fire-recovery fund involvement (Fortune, 2025-01-29). [Source](https://www.halogenvc.com/latest-news)
- How to pitch: email only — "Pitch Submissions" → hello@halogenvc.com (the mailto on the page is mistyped ".om"; use .com). No form, no stated criteria, check size, or response expectations. [Source](https://www.halogenvc.com/contact)
- Consumer portfolio named: Babylist, ThirdLove, The Skimm, Live Tinted, Flex, Trust & Will, Metropolis. [Source](https://www.halogenvc.com/)
- Community: HOP program (/hop) and a Fellowship (/fellowship) exist but are undescribed on fetched pages; events include a live podcast taping at Austin Tech Week and a Wiregrass pitch competition (undated). [Source](https://www.halogenvc.com/latest-news)

### Inferences
- Check size is unstated; a $30M fund typically writes $250K–$1M first cheques (inference, unverified).
- Halogen is the best "local champion" candidate: LA-based, press-fluent, woman-led, consumer-native. The thesis mismatch (family) is the main barrier; travel with kids / family passports could be a bridge if honest.

### Gaps
- Check size, stage definition, traction expectations — not stated anywhere fetched.
- What HOP and the Fellowship are and whether founders can apply — not described.
- Any Filipino/AAPI or solo-founder examples — not found.

[Source](https://www.grahamwalker.com/)

[Source](https://www.grahamwalker.com/pitch)

## Golden Seeds (angel network, 9 chapters, US)

### Takeaway
Large, active, women-led-only angel network ($200M invested since 2004) with a $50 application fee on Dealum — but its criteria require a product in beta plus "proof of concept" revenue, and for consumer companies $1M trailing net revenue at 40%+ gross margin. Too early for the founder; the free office hours are the only usable door now.

### Cited Findings
- Scale: founded 2004; 9 chapters, members in 28 states and internationally; 1,150+ investors; 275+ companies funded; $200M invested; $2.5B+ follow-on. Recent news items dated 2026-09-29 and 2026-10-05 show the site is maintained. [Source](https://goldenseeds.com/entrepreneurs/)
- Eligibility: "Eligible companies must have at least one woman in an operating C-suite role" with a "fair" equity stake; first-round funding; product in beta; plausible exit in 5–8 years; "proof of concept" revenue or significant pilots; consumer products need trailing-twelve-month net revenue over $1M and gross margin above 40% (or a clear path with the raise); typical first-round raise $250K–$2M; typical Golden Seeds investment $125K–$350K; pre-money generally below $10M; TAM at least $500M (consumer >$1B); US-domiciled only. [Source](https://goldenseeds.com/investment-criteria-eligibility)
- Application: via Dealum (not Gust); "There is a non-refundable $50 fee to cover our administrative costs for the application"; no fee on a reapplication; application asks for P&L, 3-year projection, funding history, valuation; Information Sessions and Office Hours offered before applying. [Source](https://goldenseeds.com/how-to-apply-for-funding)

### Inferences
- Golden Seeds is a 12–24-month-later target once there is revenue; applying now would spend $50 for a near-certain pass.

### Gaps
- Decision timeline — not stated.
- Office-hours schedule/format — not shown.
- Consumer-app or AAPI examples — not found.

## Pipeline Angels (angel network, virtual)

### Takeaway
Active (2025 Fall Cohort, 2027 Pitch Summit scheduled). Trained women and nonbinary angels pool small cheques ($5K+ each, classes of 5–10) and explicitly position as the "friends and family" round for founders who lack one; women/nonbinary founders and mission-driven for-profits are "strongly encouraged." No traction bar is stated. Next window: applications open January 2027 for the April 2027 Spring Pitch Summit.

### Cited Findings
- Network of diverse women and non-binary investors; "Angels in the Making" training program; 550+ members, 130+ member-invested companies, $7M+ invested; members "often act as the friends-and-family round for founders who lack support at that stage"; news references a "2025 Fall Cohort" and a post dated 2025-10-06. [Source](https://www.pipelineangels.org/)
- Founder eligibility: for-profit entity "duly organized and validly existing" in a US state; "Companies with a social and/or environmental mission are strongly encouraged to apply"; women and non-binary founders strongly encouraged. [Source](https://www.pipelineangels.org/forfounders)
- Process: apply at https://www.pipelineangels.org/pitch-summit-application; 2026 Fall applications closed; 2027 Spring applications open in January; East Class 2027-04-17 and West Class 2027-04-18 (virtual); 10-minute pitch + 10-minute Q&A, fireside chat, due diligence; each angel commits at least $5,000; class size 5–10; SAFE or convertible note; angels sign directly onto the cap table unless an SPV is agreed; selected companies also pitch to the 500+ member community for syndicate add-ons. No founder fees mentioned. [Source](https://www.pipelineangels.org/forfounders)

### Inferences

## 37 Angels (New York)

### Takeaway
Women-led angel network with a clear, published process (Gust application → 20-minute call → bimonthly forum, 8 of ~500 applicants pitch per cycle) and $50K–$200K network cheques — but it wants six months of customer data and most funded companies have $50K–$500K revenue. Too early for the founder; the site's 2020 copyright also raises a staleness flag.

### Cited Findings
- Mission "Closing the gender gap in startup investing through education"; investor bootcamps (NYC and online); footer "Copyright 2020." [Source](https://www.37angels.com/)
- Founder process: apply via Gust https://gust.com/organizations/37angels/apply; first step a 20-minute fit call; forums every two months (Feb, Apr, Jun, Sep, Nov); apply at least two weeks before; ~500 companies apply per cycle, 8 pitch; network invests $50K–$200K per company (individual angels ~$25K). [Source](https://www.37angels.com/founders)
- Eligibility: seed, usually raising $500K–$3M; "at least six months of customer data"; most funded companies have $50K–$500K revenue; sector-agnostic but avoids hits-based industries (retail brands, games, movies) and long go-to-market sectors; geography-agnostic but tends to US-incorporated; pitches in person in NYC; portfolio ~80% tech, 30% B2C, ~one-third women founders. [Source](https://www.37angels.com/founders)

### Inferences
- A consumer app can read as "hits-based" to them; combined with the revenue norm, a pass is likely until there is revenue.
- Fee to pitch: none stated (from memory 37 Angels has not charged founders — possibly outdated).

### Gaps

## All Raise (nonprofit, not a fund)

Useful only for its newsletter and the annual VC Summit as a place to meet women investors.

### Cited Findings

[Source](https://www.allraise.org/)
- Community eligibility: "for women and non-binary investors in venture capital at any stage of their career"; "At this time we are only able to admit women and nonbinary investors at venture firms"; Associates and above; "It's free!" [Source](https://www.allraise.org/community)
- Events: no upcoming events listed; most recent January 2025; LA items are historical (June 2023 "LA Tech Week Mixer for Female Founders & Investors", 2024 virtual/roundtables); event filters include "For Founders" but no current founder events shown. [Source](https://www.allraise.org/events/all-events)
- About page: no CEO named; no mention of Visionary Voices, VC Champions, bootcamps or office hours; donations support "key initiatives for female founders and funders." [Source](https://www.allraise.org/about)

### Inferences

- Does not invest capital (no investment program appears anywhere on the site).

### Gaps

## Fearless Fund (Atlanta)

### Takeaway
Still presents as a venture fund (pre-seed to Series A, initial cheque up to $3M, ≥10% ownership target) plus grants/programs, built "by WOC for WOC." Its own site says nothing about the lawsuit or settlement. A Filipino-American founder is a woman of colour and within the stated mission, but the ~50-company portfolio is overwhelmingly Black-women-led, there is no application form (email only), and the ownership target implies a priced round. Low odds; the grants/GVR lane is the more realistic door.

### Cited Findings
- "the 1st venture capital firm that is built by WOC for WOC"; invests in "under-resourced entrepreneurs including women of color led businesses seeking pre-seed, seed level or series A financing"; Atlanta address; nav: Venture, Loans, Africa, Grants, Partners. [Source](https://www.fearless.fund/)
- Venture criteria: team led by "at least one full-time founder"; C-Corp (or LLC on path); "At a minimum, the company should have proof of concept and preferably a minimum viable product" with some customer traction; "Our initial investment will be no larger than $3M and we seek at least 10% ownership of portfolio companies"; no application form — hello@fearless.fund; ~50 portfolio companies incl. Slutty Vegan, Bread Beauty, The Lip Bar, Live Tinted, Travelsist. [Source](https://www.fearless.fund/venture)
- Grants/programs: "Get Venture Ready (GVR) program has equipped over 600 women"; participants "pitch for grants at the end of each cohort"; 346+ grants, $5M+ in program funding; Fearless VC Summit since 2021; no mention of the Strivers Grant Contest, lawsuit or settlement; no 2025–2026 dates. [Source](https://www.fearless.fund/grants)
- Team: Arian Simone (Founder, CEO, Founding Partner); William Duckworth (Principal); Fearless Fund Africa chief of staff; advisors incl. Ben Crump; Sam Sugarman "stepped back ... in 2025" to attend Wharton; Ayana Parsons is not listed. [Source](https://www.fearless.fund/about)

### Inferences
- Litigation (from memory, possibly outdated, unverified today — Wikipedia 404'd and search was unavailable): the American Alliance for Equal Rights sued in August 2023 over the Fearless Strivers Grant Contest ($20K grants restricted to Black women); the 11th Circuit enjoined the contest in June 2024; the parties settled in September 2024 with the contest permanently ended; the fund said it would keep investing. The site's silence on this is consistent with that history.
- Ayana Parsons' absence from the team page suggests she has departed (from memory she stepped down in 2024 — possibly outdated).
- Eligibility language has shifted toward "under-resourced entrepreneurs" generally, which widens the door on paper for a non-Black founder of colour.

### Gaps
- Current fund size/vintage and whether a new fund is deploying in 2026 — not stated.
- Any 2025–2026 investments — none shown.
- Application route for GVR cohorts — not shown.

## Other women-friendly pre-seed vehicles with cold applications

### January Ventures (SF/Boston/London)
#### Takeaway
Open-application pre-seed fund that invests "before there is a product, customers or revenue," with 80% of its portfolio female-founded and a live "pitch-2026" Typeform — strong mechanical fit, though the portfolio skews B2B (AI, climate, health, manufacturing) and consumer is thin.
#### Cited Findings
- Maren Bannon and Jen Neundorfer; "invests at the pre-seed stage, before a product, customers, or revenue"; selects on "industry expertise, ambition, and tenacity rather than pedigree or network"; portfolio stats: 31% Black or Latino/a founder, 38% immigrant, 80% female founder, 68% outside the Bay Area; 60 companies, 4 exits; US and Europe; Substack newsletter. [Source](https://www.january.ventures/)
- Pitch Us → januaryventures.typeform.com/pitch-2026; "will invest in exceptional founders before there is a product, customers or revenue." [Source](https://www.january.ventures/contact-us)
- TechCrunch 2022-04-28 (possibly outdated): $21M fund; cheques $250K–$750K; sectors software incl. future of work, fintech, digital health; "two-minute survey", "No revenue, pitch deck, or warm introduction is required"; "about 18% of investments came from cold email"; 50 startups, 90% with a female founder. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/28/january-ventures-fund-3-early-stage/)
#### Inferences
- The "pitch-2026" form name indicates active 2026 deployment. Consumer travel is off their usual beat; frame the app around industry expertise (healthcare professional building a trust/identity layer for travellers) if at all.
#### Gaps
- Current fund size, response time, solo-founder stance — not stated.

### Hustle Fund (SF; not women-focused)
#### Takeaway
Pre-seed generalist that "reads everything," writes a $150K first cheque and decides in 1–2 meetings — but its FAQ requires founders to be full-time, which the founder is not today. Angel Squad (2,000+ angels) has no women-specific squad on the site.
#### Cited Findings
- "hilariously early" pre-seed software; first check in 70% of companies; decides in 1–2 meetings and wires within the week; Angel Squad 2,000+ angels; application https://hustlefund.typeform.com/to/UGTnIt. [Source](https://www.hustlefund.vc/)
- FAQ: "Our first check is $150K"; "We invest in pre-seed rounds"; geography "Mostly the United States, Canada, and Southeast Asia"; software generalists avoiding capital-intensive sectors; "We read everything"; response "between 24 and 48 hours after having a call"; founders must be full time: "Yes"; sweet spot = MVP built; Fund I $11.5M, II $34M, III $46M, IV $39M. [Source](https://www.hustlefund.vc/faq)
#### Inferences
- The full-time rule is the blocker; everything else fits. Worth applying only with a credible full-time plan.
#### Gaps
- Women-focused Angel Squad cohorts — none found; consumer stance — not stated.

## Ranking within this bucket

Ordered first by likelihood of a yes for a solo founder with ~100 users and no revenue, then by amplification power.

1. **Graham & Walker** — the only one that says solo founders are eligible, invests from "day one" at $50K–$400K, names consumer tools and human connection, and promises a two-week answer; active as of 2026-11-26. Moderate amplification (Founder Days, resources blog).
2. **Pipeline Angels** — no traction bar stated, women and mission-driven for-profits "strongly encouraged", built to be the friends-and-family cheque; applications open January 2027 for April 2027. Small money, low press.
3. **January Ventures** — invests pre-product/pre-revenue through an open 2026 form, 80% female-founded portfolio, 18% of deals from cold email; B2B tilt is the risk. Modest press (Substack, TechCrunch history).
4. **Cleo Capital** — $100K floor, "complicated consumer" thesis, open Google Form, and a GP who is on CNBC/Marketplace; vintage unverified.
5. **Hustle Fund** — reads every application, $150K, MVP stage fits; blocked by the full-time-founder rule and not women-focused.
6. **Halogen Ventures** — LA, woman-led, consumer, $30M Fund III, heavy press; thesis is "future of family" and inbound is email-only. Highest local-champion value if the fit can be made honestly.
7. **BBG Ventures** — most consumer-app-savvy portfolio (incl. a travel-itinerary app), open Typeform, "lived experience" thesis; but leads with $500K–$2M, which needs a round.
8. **Female Founders Fund** — strongest press engine in the bucket, open Typeform, geographically agnostic; but $500K–$750K seed cheques, 6–8 deals a year, and a 2026 pivot toward AI and repeat founders.
9. **Fearless Fund** — mission covers women of colour broadly, but email-only, ≥10% ownership target, and a Black-women-led portfolio; grants/GVR are the realistic lane. Litigation status from memory only.
10. **Portfolia (Rising America III)** — pre-seed "generally too early", wants a lead investor; monthly info session is free.
11. **How Women Invest** — women-founded only, $100K–$500K, but "beyond proof of concept" and B2B preference.
12. **The Helm** — late seed, $500K average; public activity signals stale.
13. **Coyote Ventures** — health-equity only; off thesis.
14. **37 Angels** — needs six months of customer data and typically $50K–$500K revenue; avoids hits-based consumer.
15. **Golden Seeds** — consumer needs $1M trailing revenue; $50 fee to apply.
16. **Astia** — its own FAQ: pre-seed is "too early"; resubmit later.
17. **Rethink Impact** — growth-stage gender-lens fund (unverified detail); no pitch form.
18. **Chloe Capital** — site effectively dark; status unverified.
19. **All Raise** — not an investor; community admits VC investors only; founder programs not on the current site.
