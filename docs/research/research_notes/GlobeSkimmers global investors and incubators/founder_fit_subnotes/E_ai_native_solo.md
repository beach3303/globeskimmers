# AI-native solo-builder programs and credits

Research date: 2026-11-26. Method note: the shared WebSearch budget for this turn was already exhausted when this agent started, so every finding below comes from direct fetches of each program's own pages (primary sources only; ~35 fetches). Several hosts returned server errors or JavaScript-only shells; those are recorded as Gaps, not guessed. Anything not confirmed from a fetched page is labelled "unverified". Fit notes refer to the generic profile: solo, first-time, woman founder; full-time healthcare job in Los Angeles; live consumer travel app on iOS + Android built alone with AI coding agents in eight months; ~100 users; no revenue; wants money and loud champions.

## a16z speedrun (SR009 / next cohort)

### Takeaway
Up to $1M ($500K for 10% on a SAFE, plus up to $500K in the next round), two 12-week in-person San Francisco residencies a year, solo founders explicitly accepted, re-applying explicitly encouraged. The next residency after the one already applied to is SR009, starting late July 2027 (Demo Day 5 Oct 2027); its application window is not yet published.

### Cited Findings
- Check and terms: "Up to $1M: $500K for 10% upfront on a SAFE, plus up to $500K more in your next qualifying round ($2M+) within 18 months." No board seat; pro rata rights and an option pool for early hires; terms "generally not negotiated". [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- Homepage: first check "up to $1M", "target a two-week close"; investing philosophy "We invest in the magnitude of a strength, not in a lack of weaknesses." [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/)
- Cadence: two residencies a year, both in San Francisco. SR008 applications ran 12 Oct – 1 Nov 2026 (11:59 PM PT); residency 1 Feb – 18 Apr 2027, Demo Day 13 Apr. SR009 starts late July 2027, Demo Day 5 Oct 2027. "If you miss a window, you can still apply and will be considered for the next residency." [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- Apply page: "SR008 runs February 1 to April 16, 2027, in San Francisco" (note the 16 vs 18 April discrepancy with the FAQ); applications after 1 Nov are considered for "the following residency"; "We review applications year-round". [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/apply)
- Location: 12-week residency is in person at the SF office; one FAQ answer says students or people with full-time jobs need about one day a week in person, which conflicts with the full-time in-person requirement elsewhere on the page. Housing not included. [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- Solo founders: "Solo applicants are accepted"; speedrun "generally prefers multi-founder teams with complementary skills, but encourages solo founders with a strong record of execution to apply"; "a technical cofounder or equivalent expertise is usually expected." [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- Re-applying: allowed; not re-reviewed within the same window, but "you can apply again for a later residency with what's changed. Many founders were accepted on a later application." [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- Sector: invests "across almost every sector, including consumer and B2B"; "the team matters more than the category." [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- Stage: "an idea, a prototype, or an early product, with a team already building"; traction not required but momentum wanted ("returning users, a design partner, a growing waitlist, or fast shipping"). [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- Decision speed: human-read, target reply 4–6 weeks, rolling; possible short video pitch or 15-minute interview; ~2-week close if accepted. [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq)
- Beyond cash: "$10M+" in credits via a Marketplace of 200+ partners; ~40 staff in GTM/talent/marketing/fundraising; "Demo Day puts you on stage in front of thousands of investors"; Talent Network of 171,000+; six months of free Ashby. [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq) [Source](https://speedrun.a16z.com/)

### Inferences
- SR008's window (12 Oct – 1 Nov) opened roughly 3.5 months before its 1 Feb start. If SR009 follows the same spacing for a late-July start, its window would fall around April–May 2027. Inference only; watch the FAQ.
- "Strong record of execution" plus "technical cofounder or equivalent expertise" is exactly the story of one person shipping two store apps and 2,000+ commits with AI agents. A re-application should lead with "what's changed" since the earlier decline (users, retention, press, ships).
- The 12-week SF residency is the real blocker for a full-time healthcare professional in LA, unless the "one day a week" allowance is confirmed by the team.

### Gaps
- SR009 application open/close dates are not published anywhere on the fetched pages.
- No published data on what share of recent cohorts were solo, first-time, or women founders, or how many were consumer apps.
- Whether a16z/speedrun writes about individual founders (blog/X features) beyond Demo Day was not visible on the pages fetched.

## Product Hunt (2026 launch mechanics, Golden Kitty)

### Takeaway
Free, maker-self-launched, scheduled up to 30 days out, live 12:01 AM PT for one day; homepage "featuring" is a hand-picked editorial decision, but unfeatured launches still go live. Weekends are explicitly suggested for "smaller teams and side projects". Golden Kitty eligibility requires a PH launch in that calendar year; the last fully described edition (2024) had "Mobile App" and "Bootstrapped & Small Teams" categories (possibly outdated).

### Cited Findings
- Anyone with a personal account can submit; makers self-launch ("you're both the hunter... and a maker"); a maker is "anyone who builds with technology", explicitly including solo developers. Company/brand accounts cannot submit. [Source](http://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734306-1-how-product-hunt-works)
- Featuring: the team "reviews every launch and hand-picks which to feature on the homepage leaderboard"; "an editorial decision"; meeting the Featuring Guidelines doesn't guarantee a spot. Leaderboard position uses upvotes, comments, time since launch and other signals; "We don't share the exact formula". [Source](http://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734306-1-how-product-hunt-works)
- What gets featured: "an editorial decision, not a checklist"; criteria originality, clear edge, plain language, it works; must be a new product or major update ("a big new feature, a new app, or a real pivot"), available to try, trustworthy maker. Unfeatured launches "still go live" and can be found via the link or "See all of today's products". [Source](http://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734341-2-what-kind-of-products-get-featured)
- Scheduling: "from tomorrow up to 30 days ahead"; live at "12:01 AM Pacific Time", runs to midnight Pacific; "There's no magic weekday"; weekends "are quieter, which may suit smaller teams and side projects"; each person can hunt up to "2 launches per day". [Source](http://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734355-3-set-your-goals-and-pick-your-launch-date)
- FAQ: submitting, commenting and Product of the Day are free; "Never pay for upvotes"; "Anyone can hunt, and we encourage makers to post their own product"; votes from "established, active community members earn your launch far more points"; relaunch allowed "Every time you ship a major update", with a relaunch request if the last launch was under two months ago; "Many launches hit their goals without topping the leaderboard." [Source](http://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734646-8-launch-faq-and-myths)
- Golden Kitty (2024 edition page, footer © 2026): "Eligible products are those which launched on Product Hunt in 2024 and follow the guidelines"; categories include Product of the Year, Mobile App, Community & Social, Personal Productivity, "Bootstrapped & Small Teams", plus Maker of the Year; winners "receive a Golden Kitty Award to display proudly". Possibly outdated for 2026. [Source](https://www.producthunt.com/golden-kitty-awards)
- The launch guide collection has eight articles (How PH works; What gets featured; Set goals/pick date; Build product page; Promote; During/after; Relaunch; FAQ). [Source](https://help.producthunt.com/en/collections/19793093-launch-guide)

### Inferences
- A solo consumer travel app gets press from PH mainly indirectly: a featured day or a Golden Kitty nomination is the asset you forward to journalists and VCs, not a guaranteed PH write-up. The "Bootstrapped & Small Teams" and "Mobile App" categories (if they recur) are the natural targets.
- Because the app has been live for months, the launch must be framed as a "major update" or "new app" to qualify for featuring.

### Gaps
- 2026 Golden Kitty categories, nomination/voting dates, and any newsletter/press mechanics: not on the fetched pages.
- Any paid placement/boost products in 2026: not mentioned on the fetched pages (the FAQ only says core actions are free).

## Cloudflare for Startups

### Takeaway
The surest "yes" in this bucket for the profile: Tier 3 is $10,000 in credits for one year, explicitly for "Bootstrapped or self-funded" companies under $1M raised, reviewed in up to 48 hours, no VC needed. Directly offsets the app's Workers/KV/D1/R2 bill.

### Cited Findings
- Tiers: Tier 3 $10K ("Bootstrapped or self-funded", under $1M raised); Tier 2 $100K (under $5M raised, "Funded by an affiliated partner"); Tier 1 $350K ($5M+, affiliated partner). "Receive up to $350,000 in credits for a year." Credits "valid for one year or until fully consumed"; no extensions. Tier 3 was raised from $5,000 to $10,000 (date not given). [Source](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/)
- Covered: Workers, Workers for Platforms, Durable Objects, Workflows, KV, D1, Queues, Vectorize, R2 (R2 capped at $2,500 for Tier 3), Pages, Images, Stream, Cache Reserve, Argo. "Effective Dec 1 and for all incoming startups: Workers AI is not covered by credits." AI Gateway and domain registration are not covered. [Source](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/)
- Eligibility: founded within the last 10 years, funded up to Series B, building a technology product, "a live and verifiable website", "an active public presence on LinkedIn, X, or GitHub", "first-time applicants only", for-profit, business email matching the domain. Excluded: agencies, consultancies, personal blogs, MSPs, resellers. [Source](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/)
- Process: apply at /lp/startups; review "up to 48 hours", then a confirmation email; upgrades case-by-case via startups@cloudflare.com. Logos shown: Perplexity, Notion, Lovable, Gamma, Robinhood, Revolut, BeReal, Opera (relationship to the program not stated). [Source](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/)

### Inferences
- The profile meets every Tier 3 criterion as written (live site, public X/GitHub presence, domain email, bootstrapped). Apply once and carefully: "first-time applicants only".
- Cloudflare's startup page shows logos, not founder stories; its press habit toward tiny startups appears limited to blog mentions. Treat it as money, not megaphone.

### Gaps
- No evidence on the page about featuring individual startups or founders; whether Tier 3 members get any blog/case-study exposure is unverified.

## South Park Commons Founder Fellowship

### Takeaway
$1M ($400K for 7% on a SAFE + $600K guaranteed in the next external round), eight-week in-person bootcamp in SF/NYC/Bangalore then an open-ended residency; solo founders welcome with "a strong bias for applicants who can build and prototype"; no industry restriction. The F26 deadline (2 Aug 2026) has passed; the next cohort's dates are not yet posted.

### Cited Findings
- /founder-fellowship redirects to /news/f26-founder-fellowship. [Source](https://www.southparkcommons.com/founder-fellowship)
- Terms: "$1M in funding" = $400K upfront for 7% on a standard SAFE + "$600K guaranteed in your next external round"; "Up to $1M in credits and perks". Eight-week in-person bootcamp late September to late November, then residency; "The Fellowship has no fixed end date." "All Fellows work from one of our offices in San Francisco, New York City, or Bangalore." [Source](https://www.southparkcommons.com/news/f26-founder-fellowship)
- Who: pre-idea OK ("you do need to show us how you generate ideas"); solo OK ("we have a strong bias for applicants who can build and prototype"); "Our only strict requirement is the ambition to build at the frontier." F26 deadline "11:59pm PT on Sunday, August 2nd"; interview decisions "by August 30th". Demo Faire events mentioned; publicity otherwise not described. [Source](https://www.southparkcommons.com/news/f26-founder-fellowship)

### Inferences
- Next fellowship is likely a 2027 cycle with a mid-year deadline (inference from F26's August deadline). The eight weeks in person conflict with a full-time job unless leave is taken.
- "Build at the frontier" language favours AI/deep-tech framing; a consumer passport app would need to be pitched as a new kind of place-memory network, not a travel utility.

### Gaps

## Apple App Store Featuring nominations

### Takeaway
A real, current, free channel open to any developer account: submit a Featuring Nomination in App Store Connect (Account Holder, Admin, App Manager or Marketing role), types App Launch / App Enhancements / New Content, minimum 2–3 weeks lead time, up to three months ahead for wider consideration. Editorial, no guarantee.

### Cited Findings
- "You can use Featuring Nominations in App Store Connect to share your new content, app enhancement, or app launch with our team." "Featuring lead time varies — please give our team a minimum of two weeks notice." "For wider featuring consideration, we recommend submitting a nomination up to three months in advance." Factors: user experience, UI design, innovation, uniqueness, accessibility, localization, App Store product page ("positive ratings and reviews"). Apple "may email your team members with Admin, App Manager, and Marketing roles with a request for promotional assets." [Source](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/getting-featured/)
- "Required role: Account Holder, Admin, App Manager, or Marketing." Types: New Content, App Enhancements, App Launch. Fields: name, type, description ("articulate the objectives of the new feature"), publish date or range, optional related apps (up to 10), platforms, regions, localizations, In-App Events, up to 5 supplemental URLs, helpful details. "minimum lead time of 3 weeks." [Source](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-featuring-nominations/nominate-your-app-for-featuring/)

### Inferences
- Nominate each major release (stamps, City Boards, video postcards) as an "App Enhancements" nomination three weeks ahead; localization and accessibility are explicit scoring factors.

### Gaps
- Acceptance odds and whether editorial favours indie/solo travel apps: not published.

## Ranking within this bucket

Ordered first by likelihood of a yes for a solo founder with ~100 users and no revenue, then by amplification power.

1. **Cloudflare for Startups, Tier 3** — the criteria are mechanical and the profile meets all of them; $10K/year on the exact stack already in production; 48-hour review. Money, not megaphone.
2. **Product Hunt launch** — a launch cannot be refused; weekends are explicitly suggested for small teams; featuring and a Golden Kitty nomination are the upside. Moderate amplification, strong as a forwardable asset.
3. **Apple App Store Featuring nomination** — free, any developer, three weeks ahead of each release; editorial odds unknown but the ceiling (an App Store feature) is the loudest free channel in this list.
4. **Z Fellows** — explicitly built for solo people with full-time jobs and side projects, mostly virtual, rolling; $10K at a $1B cap is near-grant money and the alumni list carries signal.
5. **Claude for Startups (next cohort)** — no stated solo/bootstrapped bar and the app runs on the Claude API, but the cohort is closed now and the $100K API credit needs a partner VC. Low amplification.
6. **Hustle Fund** — cold Typeform, yes/no in 1–2 meetings, wire within a week; whether they back pre-revenue consumer is unstated, so odds are unknown but the cost of asking is near zero.
7. **indie.vc** — one email, pre-revenue OK, generalist, fast decisions; but $250K+ first checks imply they need conviction in a ~100-user consumer app.
8. **Greylock Edge** — rolling, pre-idea OK, no capital required to join, $1M+ credits; showcased companies are enterprise, so a consumer travel app is off-pattern; huge champion power if accepted.
9. **a16z speedrun SR009** — re-applying is explicitly encouraged and solo founders are accepted, consumer is in scope, but a prior decline plus a 12-week SF residency against a full-time LA job make this a 2027 stretch; Demo Day to "thousands of investors" is the top amplification in the bucket.
10. **South Park Commons Founder Fellowship** — solo-friendly with a bias toward builders, $400K/7% + $600K, but eight weeks in person and a "frontier" filter; next deadline likely mid-2027.
11. **Betaworks Camp** — theme-gated (currently agents), NYC; wait for a consumer/social theme.
12. **Antler US** — solo welcome but requires a full-time resident founder in SF/NYC/Austin and is built for pre-team founders.
13. **Vercel for Startups / Replit Startup Program** — likely approvals but wrong stack; $30K / $25K only matter if infrastructure moves.
14. **OpenAI for Startups** — needs a partner-VC referral code; no.
15. **TinySeed** — B2B SaaS only; no.
16. **Ended:** Pioneer (stopped new investments 2024), buildspace ("this was buildspace").
17. **Unverified / could not be read:** HF0 (HTTP 429), Soma Capital (connection failures), Calm Company Fund (TLS error), Neo (JS-only), Sequoia Arc (all URLs 404), Lovable, Cursor, Bolt, Windsurf founder programs (404 or JS-only), Supabase and Expo startup programs (no program page found), Google Play indie apps channel (404).
