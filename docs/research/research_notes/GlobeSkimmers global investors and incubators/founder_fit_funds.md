# Founder-fit funds, angel networks and equity-free programs — for a solo, first-time, Filipino-American woman founder in Los Angeles with a live consumer travel app (as of 2026-11-26)

**Method note (read first).** Research date 2026-11-26. The shared web-search budget for this session was exhausted before any search ran, so every finding below comes from direct reads of the funds' and programs' own pages (homepages, FAQ, apply/pitch pages, portfolio pages, founders' newsletters) — roughly 250 fetches across five parallel researchers plus this coordinator. That favours primary sources but means third-party press confirmation (TechCrunch, Axios, dot.LA, Fortune) was mostly unavailable; the handful of press items cited are pages the funds themselves link to. Anything carried from pre-2025 knowledge is marked **possibly outdated**; anything a page did not state is marked **unverified**. Sites that would not render (JavaScript-only shells, 403/429, dead DNS) are named in each Gaps list rather than guessed at.

**Profile used for every fit note:** solo, first-time, woman founder of Filipino heritage; full-time healthcare professional (the company is not a health product); Los Angeles; iOS + Android consumer travel app live, built alone with AI coding agents (2,000+ commits in eight months); ~100 members; no revenue; US-incorporated assumed. Wants money **and** champions who are loud about backing founders like her. Stack relevant to credit programs: Cloudflare Workers/KV/D1/R2, Supabase, Capacitor, Claude API, Google Places API.

**Two structural constraints drive most "no" verdicts below:** (1) she keeps a full-time job, so anything requiring a full-time founder or an 8–12-week in-person residency in San Francisco is a 2027 stretch at best; (2) ~100 users and no revenue fail every angel network or fellowship with a revenue floor (Golden Seeds, 37 Angels, Tory Burch, Cartier, Astia, Harlem, Collab).

## Key question 1 — Solo-founder-friendly pre-seed funds with open applications (national funds and the LA ecosystem): status in 2026, fund size, how to apply

### Takeaway
Of the nineteen named pre-seed funds and LA programs, the ones that both take cold inbound and could plausibly say yes at ~100 users are Precursor (live submission form, "either through a mutual connection or directly"), Hustle Fund ($150K, "we read everything", but founders must be full-time), Slauson & Co.'s Friends & Family program (2026 cohort: six **consumer** companies at $300K each from 700+ applicants — the best thesis match in LA, but no application link is posted), Grid110 (free, equity-free, LA-only, virtual — a network credential, not money), Expert Dojo ($50K for 3.5% SAFE, admissions open, 24-hour review) and Wonder Ventures (SoCal-only, "no idea is too early", contact form). Bonfire, Fika and TenOneTen are B2B; Crosscut is frontier tech; Pasadena Angels excludes solo founders; Bloomberg Beta discourages cold email; First Round, PearX, Afore and 2048 are real but either write $500K+ checks or require relocation to the Bay Area.

### Inferences
- The two LA doors worth a founder's limited time are Grid110 Launchpad (likely yes; credential and network) and Slauson Friends & Family (low odds — six of 700+ in 2026 — but the best thesis match and the loudest champion). The 2026 cohort was announced on 2026-03-18, so applications for a 2027 class would plausibly open around year-end 2026 or January 2027 (inference, not stated).
- Among national pre-seed funds, Precursor is the only one with a live cold form **and** no stated full-time, relocation or B2B requirement. Hustle Fund's full-time rule, PearX/Afore FIR relocation, Bloomberg Beta's cold-email stance and First Round's check size each remove a candidate.
- Expert Dojo is the only LA program likely to write a check within weeks; its value is cash and a cohort, not amplification.
- B2B-only funds (Bonfire, Fika, TenOneTen) are still useful as ecosystem assets (newsletters, First Look Retreat, LA Venture podcast) for mapping who in LA invests in consumer.

## Key question 2 — Women-founder funds, angel networks, fellowships and grant programs (including corporate programs for women)

### Takeaway
Among women-focused equity investors, **Graham & Walker** is the standout fit — $50K–$400K "from day one", solo founders explicitly eligible, "consumer tools" and "human connection" named, a two-week response promise, and demonstrably active (posts dated 2026-11-26). Pipeline Angels (applications open January 2027), January Ventures (pre-product/pre-revenue form live) and Cleo Capital ($100K floor, "complicated consumer") are the next realistic doors. Female Founders Fund and BBG Ventures are loud and open to cold Typeforms but write $500K+ seed checks. Every revenue-gated program (Golden Seeds, 37 Angels, Astia, How Women Invest, The Helm, Tory Burch, Cartier) is a 2027–2028 target once revenue exists. The only equity-free cash she can apply for this month is the Amber Grant ($15 fee; idea-stage explicitly eligible). Visa She's Next (US), Google's Women Founders Fund (US), FedEx and Fearless Strivers are closed or past; Apple Entrepreneur Camp's page is gone.
