# Travel-industry accelerators, incubators, corporate venture programs and startup stages (status as of 2026-11-26)

Retrieval date for every item: 2026-11-26. Programs were checked against official pages where those were reachable; where a page returned 403/404 or a search snippet is the only source, that is stated. Items older than 2025 are labelled **possibly outdated**; anything not confirmed is labelled **unverified**. The web-search budget for this run was exhausted part-way through, so a handful of named programs (Airbnb, Thailand, Philippines, Heathrow, Changi 2026, Hilton/IHG direct check, Phocuswright 2027 dates) could only be partly checked; they are listed in the Gaps.

Legend for fit notes: "B2B gate" = the program selects on paying enterprise customers or pilots; "stage gate" = revenue/age/team thresholds that a solo pre-seed consumer app with ~100 users does not meet today.

---

## KQ1. Which travel-industry programs are open or opening between now and mid-2027, with dates, terms and fit?

### Takeaway
Only a few travel-specific programs are both live and realistic for a pre-seed consumer app right now: Plug and Play Travel & Hospitality (equity-free, two three-month Silicon Valley batches a year, rolling application), Intelak in Dubai (equity-free AED 50k/20k grants; 2026 round closed 21 June 2026, 2027 round not yet announced), Amadeus Travel4Impact (free, online, applications once a year from early March, but requires >2 years operating and buying customers), and the free-to-enter Global Startup Pitch 2027 (entries close 31 March 2027, final in London 24 May 2027). Most airline, OTA and GDS programs named in the brief are either dormant (Expedia Open World, Booking Booster, Qantas AVRO, Travelport Labs/Accelerator, Marriott TestBED), converted into pure VC funds (JetBlue Ventures is now SKY VC), or are climate/aviation-ops venture arms (United, Alaska, Delta) with no consumer-app lane.

### Inferences
- The app's partner-stamp revenue model (destinations, parks, airports, airlines, cafés) is itself a B2B story; packaging two or three signed or pilot "official stamp" partners before applying would convert several B2B-gated doors (Plug and Play, A4I, Intelak Wild Card, Arival) from poor fit to plausible.
- A solo founder is a hard blocker for Intelak's Accelerator (2 FT) and a soft one for Travel4Impact (2 team members incl. 1 FT); it is not a stated blocker for Plug and Play, Global Startup Pitch, Skift IDEA or UN Tourism.
- Given in-person constraints, the single best in-person commitment in H1 2027 is Phocuswright Europe London (24 May 2027) via the Global Startup Pitch, with Plug and Play Sunnyvale as the only US-based cohort program; everything else realistic is remote or entry-by-form.
