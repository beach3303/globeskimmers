# Cluster C — Los Angeles seed / pre-seed firms as candidate LEAD investors

Research date: 2026-11-26. Target: a ~$1-3M first institutional round (pre-seed/seed) for a consumer mobile travel app built in LA by a solo, first-time founder; ~100 users, no revenue, live on iOS + Android.

**Method and limits.** Ten web searches ran (one wave), after which the shared web-search budget for this session was exhausted. The rest of the research is direct reads of primary pages (firm sites, TechCrunch, dot.LA, PR Newswire). Roughly half of the firm sites were then rate-limited by the fetch safety check or are JavaScript-only (Upfront team page, Science, Chapter One, M13 approach page, Mucker FAQ, Wonder site, Greycroft/Crosscut/Amplify team pages, Struck, Techstars USC, LA Business Journal, Suster's Medium). Every field that rests on a page I could not read is marked **unverified**. Claims dated before 2025 are flagged **(pre-2025 — may be outdated)**. Nothing below is fabricated; where I could not confirm, I say so.

**Scores.** "Yes" = likelihood this firm leads a ~$1-3M round into a ~100-user, pre-revenue consumer travel app with one founder. "Wave" = size of the amplification if they did lead.

## Mucker Capital / MuckerLab

- **Accelerator terms (verified, current page):** "MuckerLab typically invests between $100,000 to $175,000 in exchange for a 10% to 15% equity stake." No fixed duration (average ~12 months, range 3-24); 20-25 companies per year in annual cohorts; "not a bootcamp," no demo days; companies graduate on agreed business/customer/financing milestones ([Source](https://mucker.com/accelerator/)). A 2021 SoCalTech report gave $100K-$200K **(pre-2025)** ([Source](https://socaltech.com/muckerlab_opens____accelerator_program_applications/s-0081407.html)).
- **Who it's for:** mainly pre-seed ("New Venture" track); applications from anywhere, no relocation required; "passionate, 100% committed entrepreneurs of any age and experience level"; "a strong preference for fully formed teams of 'builders'"; consumer companies "need more proof of product-market fit than enterprise or B2B startups"; "$0 revenue" is an accepted application state and "just a new idea on a napkin" is acceptable ([Source](https://mucker.com/accelerator/)).
- **Direct fund:** Mucker "invests in Seed and Series A companies outside the program" ([Source](https://mucker.com/accelerator/)); check size **unverified** (FAQ rate-limited, [Source](https://mucker.com/mucker-capital-frequently-asked-questions/)). MuckerLab "is not an investment bank. We do not broker financing" ([Source](https://mucker.com/faq/what-happens-after-the-muckerlab-accelerator/)).
- **Activity:** a vendor blog says "Mucker Capital backed 18 LA seed companies in 2024-2025" — **unverified** ([Source](https://www.ellty.com/blog/los-angeles-seed-investors)).
- **Inbound:** open application, "reviewing applicants on a quarterly basis," info-session webinar ([Source](https://mucker.com/accelerator/)).
- **Stance:** "100% committed" and "fully formed teams" are explicit — a founder with a full-time day job and no cofounder is pushing against two stated preferences. Not a yes/no rule, but a known headwind.
- **Fit:** Yes **4/10** for the accelerator (open door, pre-revenue OK, consumer PMF bar, team preference). It is not a $1-3M lead. Wave **5/10**.

## Cross-cutting observations

1. **LA's institutional seed firms have largely left consumer apps.** Bonfire, Fika, Act One and TenOneTen are B2B by charter; Crosscut has pivoted to frontier/defense; Upfront's 2022 sector list has no consumer-apps line; M13 says in its own words it moved "from consumer to a broader enabling technology strategy." The consumer-friendly LA checks are concentrated in **Wonder, Slauson, Amplify** (and possibly Mantis, BAM Ventures, Science — unverified).
2. **Check-size fit for a $1-3M round:** Slauson ($500K-$2M, wants to lead) and Wonder ($1-1.5M, first investor) match; Upfront ($3M+ typical), Fika ($1-6M B2B), Bonfire ($2-6M B2B), Act One ($1-2M B2B) do not fit for stage or sector.
3. **Wavemaker is not what the brief assumed.** The public firm is Singapore-headquartered, SEA-focused, enterprise/deep-tech. Manlunas is still a GP and his legacy US seeds are impressive, but there is no visible US fund to lead from. Pursue him as a person, and the firm's Philippines team as a destination-partner channel, not as the round's lead.

Wonder is the only firm with a verified quote that "no traction is not a problem." No firm in the cluster published a stance on founders with day jobs.
