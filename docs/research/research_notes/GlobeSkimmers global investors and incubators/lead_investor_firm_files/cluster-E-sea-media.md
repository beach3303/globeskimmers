# Cluster E — Southeast Asia / cross-border funds + media-amplifying investors

Research date: 2026-11-26. Candidate LEAD investors for a first institutional round (~$1-3M pre-seed/seed) into a US consumer mobile travel app (location-verified "visit stamps"), solo first-time founder in Los Angeles, Filipino heritage, full-time day job, ~100 users, pre-revenue, live on iOS + Android.

## How to read these notes

- **Research conditions.** The shared web-search quota for this session ran out after three queries, so most of this file comes from reading primary pages directly (firm sites, Wikipedia, DealStreetAsia, TechNode Global). Roughly a third of the firm sites are JavaScript shells that return nothing to a fetcher; where that happened I say so and fall back to prior knowledge, marked **UNVERIFIED (prior knowledge, may be outdated)**. Treat every unverified line as a lead to confirm, not a fact.
- **Two scores per firm.** *Fit* = likelihood of a yes to a ~100-user, pre-revenue consumer travel app with one founder who has a day job. *Wave* = size of the media/network wave if they led.
- **Pattern across the whole cluster (verified repeatedly):** almost nobody here writes a $1-3M *lead* check into a pre-revenue consumer app. The realistic shapes are (a) an accelerator check of $125K-$250K for 6-10% (Iterative, 500 Global, LAUNCH, Antler, Hustle Fund), or (b) a $250K-$1M first check from a pre-seed fund that will "set terms" but not fill the round (Hustle Fund, Cleo, Weekend Fund, Precursor). A true $1-3M lead for this profile would come from 776, Precursor, Village Global, or Day One only after meaningfully more traction.
- **Day-job blocker.** Hustle Fund states founders must be full time ([FAQ](https://www.hustlefund.vc/faq)). Every accelerator in this file is an in-person or full-time program. Expect this to be the first question from all of them.
