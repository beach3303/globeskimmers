# Cluster A — Multi-stage / brand-name firms as candidate seed LEADS

Research date: 2026-11-26. Target: lead for a ~$1–3M first institutional round into a US consumer mobile travel app (virtual passport, location-verified stamps; iOS + Android live; ~100 users; pre-revenue; one founder, LA, building full-time-adjacent).

Method: 18 web searches + ~26 page fetches. The shared web-search budget for this turn ran out partway through, so several items below are marked **unverified** rather than guessed. Anything dated before 2025 is flagged as possibly outdated. Aggregator sites (F4, CB Insights, Seedtable, StartupIntros, Peony, causo) are labelled third-party; firm pages and TechCrunch/Fortune/Dealroom are treated as primary.

Scoring convention: **Fit** = likelihood of a yes to a ~100-user, pre-revenue consumer travel app with one founder (1–10). **Wave** = size of the wave if they led (1–10).

Headline read: no firm in this cluster is a natural lead for this profile today. The three realistic doors are (1) a16z speedrun (program, not a partner-led seed), (2) CRV via Saar Gur (the only classic seed-lead GP in the cluster who publicly says he invests when "a product is only days or weeks old" and that conviction should come "not from a chart"), and (3) Sequoia Arc / Greylock Edge (open-call programs that accept pre-seed). Everyone else is a Series A-or-later relationship to start building now.
