# Candidate LEAD investors for a first institutional round (~$1-3M pre-seed/seed) — consumer travel "virtual passport" app, solo founder, Los Angeles

Research date: 2026-11-26. Scope: ~75 firms, programs and operator-angels across five clusters (multi-stage consumer, seed consumer, Los Angeles, travel-specialist, Southeast Asia + media-amplifying), plus the a16z speedrun / YC reapply mechanics and the 2025-26 consumer seed traction bar.

How this was produced: five parallel researchers (one per cluster) ran ~45 generic web searches (firm and partner names only — no company-identifying detail was used in any query) and ~180 direct fetches of primary pages (firm sites, SEC filings, TechCrunch, Skift, DealStreetAsia, Fortune, Axios). The shared search quota ran out mid-task for every researcher, so roughly a third of the firm sites were read directly and a number of items are marked **unverified** rather than guessed. Everything dated before 2025 is flagged **(pre-2025 — possibly outdated)**. The full per-firm working notes (one `##` per firm, ~176 KB) sit in five cluster files at `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-lobster-Developer-globeskimmers/7ec68930-5cee-48b7-bc69-4ec37c86ebef/scratchpad/investor-notes/` (`cluster-A-multistage.md`, `cluster-B-seed-consumer.md`, `cluster-C-los-angeles.md`, `cluster-D-travel.md`, `cluster-E-sea-media.md`) — session-scoped, copy them if they should be kept; this document is the synthesis and carries every load-bearing citation.

Scoring convention throughout: **Yes** = frank 1-10 likelihood of a yes to a ~100-user, pre-revenue consumer travel app with one founder who still holds a full-time job; **Wave** = 1-10 size of the signal/amplification if that party led.

## Key question 1 — Multi-stage and seed firms with consumer practices

### Takeaway
Across 34 brand-name and seed-consumer firms, only four publish language that permits a yes before traction — a16z speedrun, CRV (Saar Gur), Pear VC and First Round — and only Pear puts "solo founders welcome" in writing. Seven of the firms in the brief (Felicis, Craft, Coatue, Tiger, DST, Goodwater, Forerunner by AUM) are too large to lead a $1-3M round as a matter of fund math regardless of appetite; the consumer practices at Lightspeed, General Catalyst and Benchmark have each lost their consumer champion since 2025.
