# Investors with outsized media and distribution pull who write pre-seed/seed checks into consumer apps — and how a solo first-time founder reaches them (as of 2026-11-26)

Research date: 2026-11-26. Method: four parallel research streams — (A) media-operator investors, (B) celebrity/entertainment funds, (C) travel-industry founders, travel creators and consumer-product angels, (D) TV and show formats plus primary-page verification of YC, The Pitch, Shark Tank, Meet the Drapers, Unicorn Hunters and Acquisition.com. Roughly 50 WebSearch calls were available in total before the shared search budget was exhausted; the remainder of the work (~90 fetches) read primary pages directly (fund sites, application forms, SEC filings, Wikipedia infoboxes, trade press). Conventions used throughout: "possibly outdated" = source older than 2024; "unverified" = single aggregator or snippet only, or a page that could not be fetched; "invests personally" / "fund invests" / "does not invest at this stage" / "partners only" are stated per name. No figure below is recalled from memory — if it is not sourced it is listed under Gaps. Pages that blocked fetching this pass (so could not be verified): sharktankcasting.com (JavaScript-only), sevensevensix.com (JavaScript-only), drapertv.com/apply (blank), thepitch.fund (title only), bbc.co.uk and cbc.ca (Dragons' Den), Inc.com, Business Wire, Crunchbase, CB Insights, f4.fund, signal.nfx.com (partial), acquisition.com/apply (consent wall), X/Twitter (not fetchable, so follower counts are mostly gaps).

Fit notes refer to "the founder" = solo, first-time, woman of Filipino heritage, full-time healthcare job, Los Angeles, live iOS+Android consumer travel app (virtual passport, GPS-verified "I was there" stamps for 26,000+ famous places in 100+ countries, stamp art, city cards, postcards), ~100 members, no revenue, built in ~8 months with AI coding tools (2,000+ commits), ambition "stampable everywhere" then a global travel payments/tap-to-pay layer, wants an investor who can "make world waves".

Sections 1–4 below are the synthesis. Sections 5–7 are the full per-investor dossiers from the three clusters (every fact with its inline source), retained verbatim so nothing is lost in summary.

## 1. Ranked shortlist — who to pursue, in what order

### Takeaway
Only about a dozen of the ~45 names researched both (a) write pre-seed/seed checks into consumer apps today and (b) have a reachable door; of those, the three that combine a published application path with real broadcast reach are LAUNCH Founder University (Calacanis), Y Combinator (Tan) and The Pitch podcast (Muccio, with Elizabeth Yin and Charles Hudson in the room). The biggest "wave-makers" on the original list — Ohanian/776, Serena Williams' new Starfire Ventures, Mark Cuban, Gary Vaynerchuk, the Chainsmokers' Mantis — are reachable only by cold email or no public door at all; most celebrity vehicles (Sound, SKKY, MarcyPen, Plus Capital, Maximum Effort, 11:11, Dreamers, Kinship) are later-stage, partnership-only or dormant; and none of the five travel creators checked (Nas Daily, Drew Binsky, Kara and Nate, Lost LeBlanc, Eva zu Beck) is verified as an angel investor.

### Inferences
- The ranking's top three share a property none of the celebrities have: a form a stranger can fill in, a published check, and a built-in broadcast moment (TWiST speaking slots; Demo Day press; a podcast episode that airs whether or not she is funded). For a founder with no network and ~100 users, that combination beats a bigger name with no door.
- The two explicit walls are **full-time status** (Hustle Fund "Yes"; YC "commit to working full-time … during the batch and afterwards if accepted") and, softly, **solo status** (YC "more likely to succeed with a co-founder"; Paul Graham's advice shapes reviewers). Nothing fetched shows LAUNCH, The Pitch, Mantis, Cuban, Belsky, Lavingia or Starfire requiring full-time at application — those are the doors open to her while she still has a job.
- "World waves" ranking (independent of reachability): Serena Williams/Starfire and Ohanian/776 > Mark Cuban > Gary Vaynerchuk > Chainsmokers/Mantis > YC Demo Day press > Calacanis/TWiST > Lenny (product audience) > The Pitch (600K founders/investors). Reachability ranking is nearly the inverse — which is why Tier 1 is the sequencing recommendation, with Tier 2 cold emails sent in parallel because they cost nothing.
- Travel-specific signal is thin everywhere: Mantis (Atlys), Belsky (Spotnana), Paul English (Kayak, Peek), Ohanian (Hipmunk 2010), Hartz (Airbnb/Uber angel), Jay-Z (Away). No investor on the list has a 2024–2026 consumer-travel-app seed deal on record, so the pitch should not depend on a travel thesis; it should lean on consumer-collectible/social mechanics and verified-presence data.
- Southeast Asia is a named Hustle Fund geography; a Filipino-traveler-first growth plan is inside their map, which matters once the full-time wall is cleared.
