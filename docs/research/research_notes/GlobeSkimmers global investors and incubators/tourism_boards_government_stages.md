# Destination, Tourism-Board, Government, Olympics and Global-Stage Programs for a Pre-Seed Consumer Travel Startup (2026–2027)

Research date: 2026-11-26. Scope: programs that give money (equity-free or not), official partners/pilots, stage time or a media moment to a solo-founder, pre-seed, LA-based consumer travel app with ~100 users and no revenue. Everything dated before 2025 is flagged as possibly outdated; anything I could not confirm against a primary page is flagged unverified. Note on method: the UN Tourism website (untourism.int) and several others (Phocuswright, Phocuswright Europe, SXSW support, TDF, Visit California) returned HTTP 403/404 to automated fetches, so some facts below rest on search-result snippets and secondary press rather than the primary page; those are marked. My web-search budget ran out before I could cover a handful of items (listed under Gaps in each section).

Fit shorthand used throughout: **Strong** = a solo pre-seed consumer travel founder with ~100 users is squarely eligible and the program's value (partners, press, cash) matches the business model; **Medium** = eligible but the program leans B2B/enterprise or the value is mostly visibility; **Weak** = eligibility or geography excludes her, or the program is a marketing fund for destinations, not startups.

## Key Question 1 — UN Tourism (UNWTO) Global Tourism Startup Competition and other UN Tourism startup programs

### Takeaway
UN Tourism no longer appears to run a single annual "Global Tourism Startup Competition" with an open global call; in 2025–2026 its startup activity is a set of thematic and national challenges (Global Social Innovation Challenge, Morocco national competition, Ukraine Safe Destinations, Hospitality Challenge, Open Innovation Challenge France) whose national winners get a seat at a "global competition final." Prizes are mentorship, partner access and pitch slots (FITUR, Qatar), not cash. All 2026 windows found had closed by 30 September 2026; no 2027 window was published as of 2026-11-26.

### Inferences
- The MICE/Mega Events competition shows UN Tourism has run Games/mega-event-themed startup calls before; a similar theme could plausibly recur around LA28, but nothing announced confirms it.
- UN Tourism prizes are reputational and relational (a UN logo in the deck, FITUR/Qatar stage time, partner introductions). For a 100-user consumer app, the realistic value is credibility with destination partners rather than money.
- Fit: **Medium**. A US-based founder cannot enter national competitions (Morocco, Ukraine, France); the thematic global challenges (social innovation, hospitality) do not obviously match a passport-stamp product unless framed around community/heritage tourism. Watch untourism.int from January 2027 for the next global or thematic call.

- No consumer-travel-app winner from a 2025 or 2026 UN Tourism competition could be identified with a verified source.
- UN Tourism's affiliate-membership route for startups was not researched.

## Key Question 2 — National and city tourism-board startup programs and challenges (Singapore, Dubai, Saudi, Japan, Korea, Thailand, Hong Kong, Australia/NZ, UK, Spain, Portugal, Italy, France, Netherlands, US)

### Takeaway
The only tourism-board programs that are (a) genuinely open to foreign startups, (b) equity-free and (c) about pilots with destination stakeholders are the Singapore Tourism Accelerator (STB), Intelak (Emirates Group + Dubai DET), Dubai Founders HQ (DET + Plug and Play) and Saudi TDF Grow — and all four are structured around solving problem statements for hotels, airports, airlines and attractions, i.e. B2B pilots, which only fits a consumer stamp app if it is pitched as a visitor-engagement product for those venues. European tourism-board programs (Call FIT Portugal, Argo Italy, Welcome City Lab Paris) carry small cheques (€75k–€150k) but in practice require a local company. Korea, Thailand and Japan programs fund their own nationals. US DMOs (Brand USA, Visit California) run co-op marketing, not startup programs. No program below had an open application window on 2026-11-26; most run spring calls.

### Cited Findings

**Rules on Olympic marks (what a non-sponsor startup must not do)**
- Statute: 36 U.S.C. § 220506 gives the USOPC exclusive rights over the Olympic symbol, the words "Olympic," "Olympiad," "Paralympic," etc., and any indicator of association — [Cornell LII — 36 U.S.C. § 220506](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/36/220506); commentators describe it as a "super trademark" where USOPC need not prove likelihood of confusion — [IPWatchdog, 3 Aug 2021](https://ipwatchdog.com/2021/08/03/u-s-olympic-paralympic-committee-gold-standard-trademark-protection/); [Debevoise, Feb 2026](https://www.debevoise.com/insights/publications/2026/02/olympics-advertising-how-to-comply-with-the-usopcs)
- USOPC Brand Usage Guidelines (archived copy of the official page; live page at usopc.org): commercial use of USOPC IP is reserved for sponsors, suppliers and licensees; "OLYMPIC"/"PARALYMPIC" in company or product names is barred (exceptions: Olympic Peninsula, WA businesses and names in use since 1950); simulated "-lympics" names barred when they suggest affiliation; do not simulate the rings with everyday objects; do not use USOPC IP in advertising, websites, social media, merchandise, contests, team-building or fundraising; hashtags such as #Olympics, #Olympian, #TeamUSA, #Paris2024, #GoForTheGold, #GoTeamUSA prohibited on non-journalistic corporate accounts when discussing the Games; corporate accounts must not post Games images of Team USA athletes, cheer athletes using USOPC IP, or suggest any connection to USOPC/Team USA/the Games; athlete name/image use during the blackout period without a waiver can jeopardize the athlete's eligibility (Rule 40); permitted: journalistic editorial use, individuals' personal accounts, non-commercial community information, factual identification of athletes' accomplishments — [USOPC Brand Usage Guidelines (archive)](https://archive.teamusa.com/brand-usage-guidelines.html); live page — [USOPC](https://usopc.org/commercial-and-brand-usage-guidelines); Rule 40 deck (2026) says non-sponsors "can never make commercial use of Olympic and Paralympic IP" — [USOPC Rule 40 deck (PDF)](https://rule40registration.teamusa.org/Assets/doc/2026/Rule40_UpdatedDeck_081325.pdf)
- LA28-specific: USOPC has applied for or secured at least 15 federal trademark assets for LA28 including "LA28," "LA 2028" and "LOS ANGELES 2028" in any style; protected marks also include "TEAM USA," "ROAD TO LA," city-specific logos and mascots, and the motto "Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together"; ambush marketing includes displaying Olympic/athlete graphics or consistent Games hashtags in a way consumers could read as sponsorship; lower-risk alternatives: US flag, red-white-blue, public-domain imagery, reposting official USOPC content — [Spencer Fane, 3 Apr 2026](https://www.spencerfane.com/insight/never-too-early-olympic-games-2028-preparation-part-i-intellectual-property-issues/); [IPWatchdog, 1 Jun 2026](https://ipwatchdog.com/2026/06/01/olympic-games-2028-preparation-part-i-never-too-soon-to-get-your-ip-strategy-in-order/)
- USOPC enforcement has reached non-sports uses (robot-building, ferret races, video gaming) — [IPWatchdog](https://ipwatchdog.com/2021/08/03/u-s-olympic-paralympic-committee-gold-standard-trademark-protection/); USOPC stepped up enforcement ahead of Paris 2024 — [Crowell & Moring](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/usopc-ramping-up-trademark-enforcement-as-paris-summer-olympics-approach)

### Inferences
- A venue-list of "LA 2028 Olympic venues" inside an app is commercial use of a protected term; the safe pattern is to name the venues themselves (SoFi Stadium, Crypto.com Arena, Rose Bowl) and the sport, and to describe them as "2028 Games venue" only in factual/editorial copy — and even that should be reviewed by counsel given the no-confusion-required standard. Stamp art must not use rings, torch, emblem, mascot or the LA28 wordmark (consistent with the founder's existing art rule).

There is no public LA28 startup accelerator to apply to.
- The Paris precedent suggests LA28's innovation activity, if any, will be curated and venue-operational (crowd flow, sustainability, accessibility) — a consumer stamp app would need a venue or city sponsor to be tested.

### Gaps
- LA28's own brand-protection page for businesses (la28.org) could not be searched (budget); the above relies on USOPC and law-firm sources.
- Whether LA28 will run any innovation/pilot program in 2027 — no announcement found.
- Intuit IDEAS LA28 2027 round — not announced on the FAQ.
