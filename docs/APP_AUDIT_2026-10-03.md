## P1 — the engagement spine (the "lonely Home" bill of particulars)

6. **No signal channel exists for "someone saw you."** Zero push infra, zero
   badges, zero unread marks anywhere. Blotter reactions, guestbook
   signatures, co-signs, postcards TO you, follow requests, tag invites — all
   invisible until the user stumbles onto the right page. Cheapest first
   step: unread dot on the Mailbox tab (feed already has timestamps; one
   lastSeen marker). Real fix: push notifications (post-approval build).
7. **Home is socially silent.** No feed teaser, no "2 new reactions on your
   Atlanta page," no pending-request nudge. Day-2 Home ≈ day-1 Home (one
   daily quote rotates). This is the Friends' Travels zone from the
   2026-10-02 brainstorm — Mailbox already has the feed; Home never
   advertises it.
8. **Cold-start social has no on-ramp:** follow requires typing an @handle;
   claiming YOUR handle is two hops away (Settings); InviteButton is hidden
   until a handle exists — the one action a zero-friend user can take is
   gated behind the thing they haven't done. FIX: inline handle claim in
   Mailbox/Profile; show invite at zero-follow.
9. **Out-of-coverage Home collapses the thesis:** StampsNearYou renders null
   without owned-DB coverage, which also starves PassportHero's "NEXT STAMP"
   line — in uncovered cities the first screen shows no collectible at all.
   FIX: fallback card (nearest city set at distance / home-city stamp).
10. **ActivityDetail buries the stamp:** "📍 Stamp it — I'm here" sits below
    hero/rating/hours/Directions — typically one swipe under the fold; the
    Guestbook tab shows no entry count. The memory layer hides behind
    utility chrome.
