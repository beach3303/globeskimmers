The Reframe

Travel is episodic. We've been building for the 5%.

A directory is only valuable while you're standing in an unfamiliar city. But the happiest, most frequent travel emotion — daydreaming about the next trip — happens on the couch, 350 days a year. Studies show planning a trip boosts happiness more than the trip itself. We invest in the phase with the least emotional charge and under-invest in the one with the most. Reversing that is the whole game.

What Makes It Stick

The loop — not any single feature — is the unicorn.

Each piece deposits something that pulls the user back later. A one-off task becomes a habit that beats on the 350 non-travel days.

🔎 Find→ ❤️ Save→ 💭 Dream feed resurfaces it→ 🛎️ Book→ ⏳ Countdown→ 📍 On the ground→ 🛂 Memories→ 🕰️ "1 year ago today"↻

Three engines drive the loop between trips: a Living Homepage that sells the next trip daily, a Passport that shows progress toward what you haven't done yet (not just a log), and memory resurfacing that re-triggers the dream.

The Signature Magic

A search that understands needs, not keywords.

It parses every query into structure, routes to exactly one surface, then answers → lets you act → anticipates the next need. Two fields are the moat: where-scope (the “near my hotel” trick, powered by Your Stay) and intent-mode (present vs planning — the demand-graph gold).

The Honest Diagnosis

Why v1 felt like a pile of tools — and why that's fixable fast.

  • A 29-tile launcher. Every tile has equal weight — nothing says “this is for you, here, now.” It's a directory, not an assistant.
  • Search was removed from the nav and re-built separately inside ~24 finders — so to search anything, you first have to know which tool owns it. That is literally why it feels like separate tools.
  • “Your Stay” is buried as a mid-feed strip instead of the hub everything radiates from.
  • The retention hooks are hidden — Passport, My Trip and Wishlist only appear after you've already engaged.

The good news: the “AI-smart brain” already exists in the code — a full context-ranking engine sitting 90% unwired. This is a wiring job, not a research project.

The Path There

Phased build — biggest felt leap first, near-zero new cost.

1

The Spine + the Living Homepage

Ship the one search box that routes into the finders we already have, and wire the dormant brain so the home leads with a ranked, explained feed (“Rainy evening, just landed in Lisbon → dinner · money · ATM”). Finders retreat to a tab.

Highest leverage · ~0 new Places spend · fills the moat day one
2

The “Your Stay” hub + present/planning

A guided “Where are you staying?” flow (find your hotel by name, not the generic picker), made the authoritative center everything re-centers on. Present vs planning changes the verbs (“Go now” vs “Save to trip”).

Turns on the signature “near my hotel” magic
3

The Loop — retention between trips

Passport as forward-looking progress (“12 of 195 countries”, near-completion nudges, an honest non-punishing streak). Memory resurfacing (“1 year ago today in Bangkok”). A My-Trip countdown dashboard for booked trips — the daily-open magnet.

Attacks the dormancy problem — the real churn
4

Full AI reasoning + earned notifications

Haiku parsing for messy queries, AI answer cards with honest sourcing + next-step chips, anticipatory nudges, and notifications that are only ever about your stuff or genuinely time-sensitive.
