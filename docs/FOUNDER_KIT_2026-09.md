## Prep app intake answers

1. **What is the business called?** Globeskimmers.
2. **What does it sell?** A free iPhone travel app that finds what you need abroad and books the stay from home. The thing it sells is a hotel room booked inside the app; tours, transfers and rental cars are referred to partners.
3. **Who actually pays you?** The traveler who completes a hotel booking in the app. Nuitée, the booking partner, is the merchant of record and pays Globeskimmers a commission on each booking. Tours, transfers and cars pay referral fees. The traveler never pays Globeskimmers directly today.
4. **What stage are you at?** Pre-revenue.
5. **What do you charge, and how?** The app is free. Rooms are sold at the partner's rate and Globeskimmers earns a commission per booking, set in the partner dashboard. Referral fees on tours, transfers and cars. No subscription, no paid tier.
6. **What do you do there?** Founder and builder. Product, design, code, data, the store listing and support, all mine, built with heavy AI assistance.
7. **Who do you sell to?** Individual consumers. B2C, products people buy: today a hotel room, later a package around it.
8. **What problem do you solve, in their words?** "I'm in another country and I need a restroom, an ATM and somewhere decent to eat right now, and I don't trust the reviews." "Every site sells me one piece of the trip and hides what's not included." "Nobody asks whether the scooter fits in the van."
9. **What do they want instead?** One app that answers "what's around me" honestly, nearest first, and one place to plan and book the stay with the truth about what's included and what isn't.
10. **Why you, rather than the obvious alternative?** Honesty as a product rule: a middling match is called middling, ratings carry Google's name, every booking names its partner, accessibility is never guessed. An owned database of about 75 million places keeps the free product free. And I build it for the trips my own family takes, including someone on a power scooter.
11. **What proof do you have?** iPhone app live on the App Store since July 8, 2026. Website live September 21, 2026. In-app hotel booking in production since September 5, 2026, in the current build; the App Store release carrying it is next. Owned database of about 75 million places. 843 commits since March 2026, one author. User, download and booking counts: unknown, and I won't quote them until they're real.
12. **What do you want more of in the next 90 days?** Sales. Completed hotel bookings inside the app, starting with the first one. The store release that puts booking into every download is the enabler.
13. **How do they get it?** Checkout inside the app on the partner's hosted payment page. The confirmed stay lands in My Trip with the partner named.
14. **What is the next step you want them to take?** Download the app and book your next stay in it. If the trip isn't booked yet, try the finders abroad and tell me what's wrong.
15. **What do they do today instead?** Five apps and a group chat: Google Maps for what's nearby, Booking or Agoda for the room, Viator for tours, a family thread for the plan, and guessing about accessibility.
16. **How do you sound when you talk about this?** Warm, plain, direct. Specific beats clever. I say what's not included before what is.
17. **What should the Brain never say?** No user numbers, downloads or revenue we can't show. Never that flights are sold in the app, or that it's on Android. Never "guaranteed", "best", "ADA compliant" or "accessible" without a source. Never promise a reply or a booking we can't make. No hype words, no jargon, no em dashes.
18. **Say it the way you would say it to a customer.** Here's what it does, plainly. In another country it finds what's around you, nearest first: food by the dish, coffee, a restroom, an ATM, a hotel for tonight. At home you name a destination and dates and it shows two hotels side by side at real rates, booked inside the app. If a match is middling, it says middling. If accessibility isn't confirmed, it says so. I built it for the trips my own family takes. Website: globeskimmers.io

- A team, a company entity, funding or investors. Solo founder, no entity documented.

- Anything with the word guaranteed, or a promise of a reply.
- Names, ages or conditions of family members. Say "two older relatives I'm close to" and stop there.
