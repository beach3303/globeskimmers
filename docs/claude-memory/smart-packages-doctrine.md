Founder's Smart Packages doctrine (brainstormed 2026-09-03, refines [[google-of-travel-brief]]):

**Three audiences, distinct shelves:** (1) dreamer at home — buys full packages + price watches; (2) traveler on the ground — nearby services, eSIM, events, urgent hotel-tonight, DAY PACKAGES ("Statue of Liberty in one day" = bookable ticket + transit steps + timing); (3) curious browser — atlas/stamps, the funnel into (1).

**The two-choice rule:** "give them less chance to select and change their mind" — exactly TWO hotel choices (top two; king or two queens). Budget tier = a SEPARATE package variant (cut the transfer/rental, never the bags or the experience). Business class = separate variant. Party size changes the package, not a per-person teaser.

**Pre-decided defaults:** round-trip air with 2 checked bags ALWAYS included; arrive-rested flight timing; last mile (seaplane/boat/bus) pre-booked inside the total; minimum trip length stated ("you need 3 days"). "We do most of the thinking and all they do is select and purchase."

**Why-explanations everywhere:** suggestions must say WHY — closer to you, most fun per reviews, saves you time, beats the traffic window.

**Installments:** founder said Klarna (apply alongside [[nuitee-production-flip-pending]]'s Uplift plan — Klarna onboards faster).

**Why:** the founder's own words repeatedly: mind-blowing experience, clean categories per audience, sell confidently while making days smoother.
**How to apply:** every package/planner surface built for GlobeSkimmers follows the two-choice rule + pre-decided defaults; day-package lane is the bridge from nearby services to commerce.
