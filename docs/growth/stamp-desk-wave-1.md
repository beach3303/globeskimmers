# Stamp Desk — wave 1: six launch posts

For Facebook Reels, TikTok and Instagram Reels. Audience: Filipino travelers (growth plan, section 3).
Drafted 2026-10-20. Post by hand from the brand accounts, one every two or three days.

**How to use this page**
- Each post is one vertical video, 9:16, 15–25 seconds, recorded on your phone. Use the screen recorder for app shots.
- One video, three platforms. Post the same cut to all three, and change only the link code.
- **Links in the bio, not in captions.** On Reels and TikTok a link in the caption usually isn't tappable. Point the bio link at `globeskimmers.io/get?src=fb`, `?src=ig` or `?src=tiktok` per platform, so
  Admin can see which one brought people in.
- **Music:** pick from each app's commercial library (Facebook Sound Collection, TikTok Commercial Music Library,
  Instagram's "for business" sounds). Brand accounts can't use regular trending songs.
- **Before posting:** check every Taglish line reads naturally to you. You know the voice better than I do.

**Rules for every post**
- Real stamps and real screens only, recorded from your own passport or a test account that you made yourself.
- Nothing live: show trips that are over. Never show where anyone is right now.
- No one under 18 on camera.
- No contests, no "comment YES to…", no "share to win".
- No Disney, Universal, FIFA or Olympic names or logos.
- Say what a green check means honestly: proof from your phone's location, your photo's location tag, or the place
  recognised in your photo.

## 1 · "Your last trip, as passport stamps"
**The camera-roll promise. Post this first.**

- **Hook, on screen (0–2 s):** "POV: you finally do something with your 2,000 Japan photos"
- **Shots:**
  1. (0–3 s) Scroll fast through a camera roll full of trip photos.
  2. (3–8 s) In GlobeSkimmers: Passport → **Upload photo to get a stamp** → select 15–20 photos.
  3. (8–15 s) The app groups them by place and day and offers stamps. Tap through two or three.
  4. (15–20 s) The stamps land in the passport book. Flip one page, and show a green check.
- **On-screen text, in beats:** "Pick your trip photos" → "It finds where each was taken" → "Stamps for the famous
  places" → "Back to 2021, so old trips count too"
- **Caption:**
  > Turn your trip photos into passport stamps 🛂 Pick photos from any trip since 2021, and GlobeSkimmers finds where
  > each was taken. Free on iPhone and Android, link in bio.
  > Yung Japan trip mo last year? Gawin na nating passport. ✈️
- **Hashtags:** #pinoytraveler #travelph #japantrip #passportstamps #globeskimmers

## 2 · "Guess the stamp: Japan"
**A quiz. People watch twice to check their answers, and the platforms reward that.**

- **Hook, on screen:** "Guess the Japan stamp in 3 seconds 👀"
- **Shots:** five stamp arts, one at a time, each blurred for 2 seconds, then sharp with its name. Use **Mount Fuji,
  Fushimi Inari Shrine, Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), Tokyo Tower, Osaka Castle**. Record the blur with the phone
  editor's blur, or zoom the stamp from small to full.
- **End card (2 s):** "Collect them by being there. GlobeSkimmers, free."
- **Caption:**
  > How many did you get? 🇯🇵 Every famous place in Japan has its own stamp, and you earn it by being there or with
  > your own photo from the trip.
  > Ilan ang nakuha mo? 😄
- **Hashtags:** #japan #japantravel #pinoysajapan #guessthestamp #globeskimmers
- **Note:** "How many did you get?" is a question, not a demand to comment, so it stays clear of engagement-bait
  rules. Don't add "comment your score".

## 3 · "The Philippines, 15 stamps"
**Local pride. The most shareable post for the beachhead.**

- **Hook, on screen:** "15 Philippine stamps. How many have you earned?"
- **Shots:** a fast montage, one second each, of the 15 illustrated stamps: Chocolate Hills, Mayon Volcano, Banaue Rice
  Terraces, El Nido, Boracay, Intramuros, Puerto Princesa Underground River, Taal Volcano, Kawasan Falls, Vigan,
  Sagada, Coron, Mount Pinatubo, Siargao, Panglao Island. End on a passport page with three or four of them stamped.
- **Caption:**
  > 15 Philippine icons, each with its own passport stamp 🇵🇭 Which ones are in your passport, and which are next?
  > Save this for your next long weekend. Free app, link in bio.
  > Saan ka next? 🏝️
- **Hashtags:** #itsmorefuninthephilippines #travelph #pinoytraveler #philippines #globeskimmers
- **Timing:** good before a long weekend or Holy Week. "Save this" is fine, because it asks for nothing in return.

## 4 · "Hong Kong and Singapore, the checklist"
**For the two most common short trips.**

- **Hook, on screen:** "Hong Kong + Singapore: the stamps you can collect"
- **Shots:** split into two halves. **Singapore:** Marina Bay Sands, the Merlion, Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa, the
  Singapore Flyer. **Hong Kong:** Victoria Harbour, the Tian Tan Big Buddha. Show each stamp with a ✓ appearing, like a
  checklist being ticked.
- **On-screen text, at the end:** "Plus the airport arrival stamp the moment you land ✈️"
- **Caption:**
  > Weekend in Singapore or Hong Kong? These are the stamps waiting for you, plus an arrival stamp at the airport.
  > Free on iPhone and Android, link in bio.
  > Para sa susunod na lipad. ✈️
- **Hashtags:** #singapore #hongkong #pinoytraveler #travelchecklist #globeskimmers
- **Check first:** stamp the airport arrival on the demo or your own account, so the last shot is real. Hong Kong only
  has two illustrated stamps today, so keep it to those two.

## 5 · "4 words max"
**Telegrams. Something no other travel app has.**

- **Hook, on screen:** "You get 4 words. Make it count."
- **Shots:**
  1. Mailbox → **Telegram** → pick a friend.
  2. Type "Landed. Miss you!", then try a fifth word and watch the app refuse it.
  3. Tap **Wire it**.
  4. Cut to the other phone: a sealed envelope with a wax seal, "Tap to open", the seal breaks, and the telegram
     slides out with its RECEIVED stamp.
- **On-screen text:** "4 words max" → "It arrives sealed" → "They open it to read it"
- **Caption:**
  > Old-school telegrams, but in your pocket 📮 Four words, a sealed envelope, and a RECEIVED stamp when they open it.
  > Send one to your barkada. Free, link in bio.
  > Apat na salita lang. Kaya mo ba? 😆
- **Hashtags:** #telegram #barkada #pinoy #travelbuddies #globeskimmers
- **Note:** film it between two of your own accounts (yours and the demo, or two phones). Show no one else's real
  username.

## 6 · "Send a postcard, not a story"
**Postcards: private and personal.**

- **Hook, on screen:** "Stories disappear. Postcards don't."
- **Shots:**
  1. Mailbox → **Postcard** → pick a trip photo → Edit, our looks: "Golden Hour" → write a line → send to one person.
  2. Cut to the recipient's Mailbox: the postcard arrives with its postmark and stamp.
- **On-screen text:** "Your photo on the front" → "Your words on the back" → "Only they can see it"
- **Caption:**
  > Instead of posting it for everyone, send it to the one person who should see it 💌 Your trip photo on the front,
  > your words on the back, sealed in their mailbox.
  > Para kay Mama, kay Lola, sa tropa. ❤️
- **Hashtags:** #postcard #travelmemories #pinoytraveler #ofwlife #globeskimmers
- **Note:** "Only they can see it" is true. The mailbox is private, and a postcard to one person reaches only them.
