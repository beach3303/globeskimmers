Delete your account

Globeskimmers · Developer: Maiza Simeon

In the app

  1. Open Globeskimmers and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Deletion starts as soon as you confirm. We delete your account and profile, your sign-in history, your passport stamps and their photos, the travel-buddy tags you sent or received, your passport share link, and your guestbook photos and visit records.

Some things stay. Guestbook tips you posted stay up with your name, home city and photo removed, shown as "A traveler". Records of hotel bookings and of partner links you tapped stay in our booking records, tied to an internal ID but not to your name or email. Places you saved on this device stay on the device until you delete the app.

By email

If you can't sign in, email founder@globeskimmers.io from the address you signed up with. We confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel any booking. A hotel booked inside the app is held by Nuitée Travel Ltd, our booking partner and the merchant of record, and stays booked. My Trip goes with your account, so before you delete, keep Nuitée's confirmation email and note the booking reference shown in My Trip. For help with a stay booked in the app, email founder@globeskimmers.io. Bookings made on a partner's site, like a tour on Viator or a car on Discover Cars, are held by that partner under their own terms.
