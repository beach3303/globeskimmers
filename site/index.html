How it works

Dream it. Stamp it. Keep it.

01 · Dream it

Find the places worth going

Pick any city and see its icons, its customer favorites and the day trips worth taking.

02 · Stamp it

Be there, and it’s yours

Famous landmarks, airports and new countries all have stamps. The green check means you proved it: your phone was there, or your photo shows it.

03 · Keep it

Your photos, on the page

Pin your photos to every stamp and share the page. Your circle can react with stamps, sign your guestbook or send you a postcard.

Our promises

Honest by design

  • A check that means something.

    The green check means your phone was there, your photo’s location puts you there, or the place is clearly in your photo.

  • Just you and your inner circle.

    That’s the default: the people you follow who follow you back. Your stamps show the place and the date, never where you stood. Go public, or hide any stamp, whenever you like.

  • It’s free.

    Start your passport on iPhone today. Android is on the way.
