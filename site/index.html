What's inside

What the app does today.

Near you now

The mundane, sorted nearest first

Places to eat, coffee, ATMs, restrooms, convenience stores, money exchange, shopping and things to do, ordered by distance from wherever you stand, plus events and ways to get around. Search restaurants by the dish, not the category: every match carries a plain tier, and when nothing nearby fits, the app says so. Basic phrases with audio, cultural notes and a camera that reads a price tag or a menu cover the moments a map can't.

Virtual Passport

Stamps you actually earned

Land at an airport, stand at a famous place, cross a border, or reach a filming location, and its stamp is yours — dated the day you were there. The green check means it's verified: by GPS at the place, or by your photo's own location. No check-ins from the couch.

Collect memories

Your photos, on the page

Pin your memory photos to any stamp — the whale shark, the plate of shrimp and grits, the view from the steps — and the page becomes a travel album. Share it to Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat or anywhere else, sized right for stories and posts.

Dream and plan

Galleries for the places you'll go

Credited photo galleries of places you haven't been yet, a Perfect Day planner that builds a day around what's near you, and a wishlist that keeps every dream until you stamp it.

Virtual Passport

Collect the world, one stamp at a time.

Every Globeskimmers account comes with a Virtual Passport. Land at an airport, reach a famous place, or cross into a new country, and the app offers you a stamp dated the day you were there. Add your own photos to each stamp and it becomes a travel album too, a book of places and the moments you had there.

For partners and investors

Globeskimmers is built and run by one founder, Maiza Simeon. The product is the Virtual Passport — beautiful, verified travel stamps with the traveler's own memory photos — wrapped in the nearby finders every traveler needs. It runs on an owned worldwide places database with Google as a paid fallback, is free and ad-supported, and partners with attractions, tourism boards and event venues on official stamps, labels and visitor insight.
