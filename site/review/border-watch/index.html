Border Watch — foreground location service

Globeskimmers for Android, version 1.0.13 (16). This page is for app review.

The recording shows: Settings → Virtual Passport → the switch Watch for borders on this trip turned on by the user; the switch reads “On until … 2:45 PM”; the notification shade with the ongoing notification Border Watch is on · Until … · your location stays on your phone and its Stop action.

What the service does:

  1. It starts only when the user turns the switch on, and only for 24 hours; it also stops from the notification’s Stop action or the switch.
  2. Every few minutes it takes a location reading and checks, on the device, whether the phone has crossed into a different US state or country.
  3. When it has, it shows one notification (“You crossed into Arizona — Your border stamp is ready in Globeskimmers — it waits 48 hours”). Opening the app lets the user add the crossing to their travel passport.
  4. No location leaves the device unless the user taps to add the stamp.

Permission used: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION (task: geofencing). No background location permission is requested.
