Privacy Policy

Globeskimmers · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Globeskimmers ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Globeskimmers mobile application and related services (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you create an account or sign in using email, Google, Facebook, or Apple, we collect your name (or first name) and email address to create and secure your account, and to show your first name on guestbook notes you sign and on your shared passport.
  • Home and travel details. You may tell us your home city and country, which personalize the App (for example, which stamps count as a trip). Earlier versions of the App also asked optional questions such as travel budget or accommodation style; if you answered them, those answers stay in your account until you delete it or ask us to remove them.
  • Location data. With your permission, we access your device's approximate or precise location to find places near you (restaurants, ATMs, coffee shops, restrooms, stores, and more) and to provide weather and local information. You may instead enter a location manually, and you can disable location access at any time in your device settings.
  • Stamps and your location. A stamp is earned by being at the place. When you tap Stamp, the App reads your device’s location once and checks it, on your device or (for attractions) on our server, against the place’s own location. Our server also compares the country of your internet connection and how fast you would have traveled since your last stamp, to catch spoofing. For a stamp at a named place, your position is used only for that check and is not stored; the stamp keeps the place’s location and the date. Two kinds of stamp keep a location from your device: a stamp for crossing into a new country, state or region keeps the point where your phone was, and a “Stamp a place” stamp where you don’t pick the city or spot from the search list keeps the location the App was using when you added it, which may be where you are now rather than the place you visited. If you prove a stamp with a photo, the App sends the location and time saved in that photo to our server for the check; they are not stored, and photos are saved without their location tags. Other people never see these coordinates, with one exception: a travel buddy you tag on a stamp receives that stamp’s map point. Otherwise they see only a stamp’s name, city, country and date, plus any photos or note you choose to show, and only for stamps you haven’t hidden.
  • Saved & recent locations. Places you save, and your wishlist, are stored in your account with their locations so they follow you to other devices. Recently used locations are stored on your device.
  • Past partner-link taps. Before October 3, 2026 the App had links to booking partners. Each tap was recorded with a click ID, your account’s internal ID if you were signed in (never your name or email), the partner and product tapped, and the destination city and country. We keep those records for accounting, including after you delete your account.
  • Website messages. If you write to us through the contact form on globeskimmers.io, we store your name, email, company if you give one, which kind of sender you are (traveler, partner, investor, press), your message, the page you sent it from, a hashed version of your IP address (used to limit spam) and your browser’s user-agent string (browser, version and operating system). We use them to reply to you and to prevent spam, and the Globeskimmers team reads these messages in our admin tools. We keep a message until we no longer need it to reply or keep a record, and we delete one on request.
  • Profile & social information. If you use the Virtual Passport's social features, we collect the profile you choose to create: a username (handle), display name, "about me" line, website, favorite places, future destinations, and an optional profile photo. We also collect your date of birth, together with the country your internet connection is in when you enter it (so we can apply local age rules), to apply age-appropriate protections — see Section 9 — and, only in aggregate (never individually), to understand the age range of the people who use GlobeSkimmers. Your birthday is never shown on your profile or passport. If you tag a friend by email, we store that address to deliver the tag; we don’t email it. If you ask us for a username or our verified Seal, we store your request and email.
  • Content you share. The App stores the travel content you create: passport stamps and their notes, memory photos, postcards, signatures, drawings and reactions on friends’ passports, guestbook notes, dish and drink photos you post at a place, private messages you send to a place’s owner, and your virtual luggage and travel-buddy items. By default your passport is visible only to you and your friends (people you follow who follow you back); it becomes visible to others only if you make it public or share a page. You can hide any stamp so that only you see it. Guestbook notes are different: they appear on the place’s guestbook for every App user, under your first name and, if you choose, your home city. Dish photos are shown without your name. Messages to a place’s owner are delivered to that business once it verifies the place, and carry your first name only if you choose to sign.
  • Usage information. To understand and improve the App, we record how it is used: screens opened, places and cards you tap or save, which maps app you open for directions, time spent on a screen, how long a session lasts, the searches you type, the city and country the App is showing, the kind of device (for example iPhone or Android), and, if you pick one, who you’re traveling with (solo, couple, family or friends). These records are linked to your account and to a random ID created when the App is first opened on a device. We delete them after 180 days and keep only daily totals by city and country. We also keep a list of the random IDs we’ve seen, so we can tell when a new phone opens the App. The App shows no advertising and collects no advertising identifiers.
  • App updates. Our update service, Capgo, receives a random device ID, your device platform, the App and operating-system versions, when the App is opened or moved to the background, and whether an update installed or the App closed unexpectedly. It uses these to deliver updates and tell us when something breaks. This is not linked to your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, personalize, and improve the App's features (nearby search, translations, weather, cultural information).
  • Create, authenticate, and maintain your account.
  • Check photos, drawings and text for safety before others can see them, and confirm a stamp from a photo you choose to submit.
  • Tell our team about activity such as a new download or a new stamp (the place, city and country; never your name, and never a stamp that is private when it is made). Our admin tools also count stamps by place, city and country without showing who made them.
  • Send service-related emails.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.

3. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the App:

  • Google — Places and Maps (finding places near the location you search from), text-to-speech for language phrases, and Sign-In. Your use of these features is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic — its Claude AI service checks photos, drawings and much of the text you post for safety (see Section 8), recognizes the place in a photo you submit to confirm a stamp, translates phrases, and helps understand what you type in search.
  • Supabase — our database and sign-in provider. It stores your account, home and preference details, date of birth and profile, saved places, stamps and the things you post or send, and sends sign-up and sign-in emails.
  • Cloudflare — runs our servers and stores your photos, usage records and website messages.
  • Capgo — delivers App updates (see "App updates" above).
  • Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) — weather for a location, and place search when you add a stamp for a spot by hand. They receive the location or text needed for that request, not your identity.
  • Maps, images, fonts and flags — some content loads directly from public services: map images from OpenStreetMap and CARTO, fonts from Google Fonts, images from Wikimedia, and flags from flagcdn. Like any website, they see your device’s IP address and the map area or item requested, but not your account. When you ask for directions, the App opens Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze with your start and end points.
  • ntfy and Twilio — deliver activity notices to our team (see Section 2), without your name.
  • Apple and Facebook — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Resend — sends our emails: invitations from our team to people we’ve reserved a username for, letters telling an account it has received our verified Seal, notices to our team about guestbook notes awaiting review, and messages about hotel stays booked in the App before October 3, 2026.
  • Nuitée Travel Ltd — the merchant of record for hotel stays booked in the App before October 3, 2026. For those bookings it holds the guest name, email and stay details it needs to keep the reservation.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

4. Advertising

The App contains no advertising. We do not use advertising SDKs, do not collect or share advertising identifiers, do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and do not sell or share your information with data brokers.

5. No Bookings or Affiliate Links

As of October 3, 2026, the App does not sell or book anything and contains no affiliate or partner booking links. Prices shown in the App — for example admission or parking — are there to help you plan, with their source named; to buy, you go to the venue or its official website yourself.

Bookings made before that date. A small number of hotel stays were booked in the App earlier, sold by Nuitée Travel Ltd as merchant of record (Nuitée charged the card and holds the reservation; Globeskimmers never stored card details). We keep the booking record — hotel, stay dates, booking reference, and the guest name, email and phone entered for the stay — so we can help with the stay, cancellations and refunds. It is kept after you delete your account.

6. Data Retention & Deleting Your Account

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Usage records are deleted after 180 days.

You can delete your account in the App (Settings → Delete Account); globeskimmers.io/delete-account explains how, and you can also email us. Deleting removes your account, sign-in history, home and preference details, passport stamps and their photos, travel-buddy tags, share link, saved places and wishlist, and guestbook photos. Guestbook tips you posted stay up, shown as “A traveler”. Some data is not yet removed automatically: your social profile (including your date of birth and profile photo), follows and blocks, postcards, signatures and reactions you left on friends’ passports, dish posts, messages to place owners and usage records. Email founder@globeskimmers.io and we will delete them within 30 days. Some information may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes, such as the booking and partner-link records described above.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your account information by contacting us at the address below. You can also delete your account yourself in the App (see Section 6).
  • Location: grant or revoke location access in your device settings, or use a manually entered location.

8. User Content, Moderation & Safety

  • You and your friends by default. Your Virtual Passport and its photos are visible only to you and your friends unless you make them public or share a page, and any stamp can be hidden so only you see it. Your username, display name and profile photo appear next to things you post or send (postcards, comments) and to people you follow or who follow you.
  • Automated review. Photos, drawings and postcard photos are reviewed automatically, using Anthropic’s Claude, before they can be shown to other people; content that fails review is not displayed publicly. Guestbook notes, shared stamp notes, photo captions, comments, dish names and messages to place owners are screened for abuse the same way before others see them.
  • Reporting & blocking. Shared passports and guestbook notes can be reported in the App, and you can report anything else — a profile, a postcard, a comment — by emailing founder@globeskimmers.io. Reports are reviewed by our team. You can block someone who tags you in a stamp, and blocking is silent; to stop anyone else from contacting you, email us and we will block them. Owners can remove any reaction or comment left on their pages.
  • Removal. To remove content you created, delete it in the App or contact us and we will remove it.

Sensitive places

You can stamp any place you really visit, including places such as hospitals, clinics, schools, shelters and places of worship. The App never asks why you were there. When we recognize a place like this, from its Google place type or from the name you type, the stamp starts private and visible only to you, and it is left out of our team’s activity notices. You choose whether to share it. Recognition is automatic and can miss a place, and famous landmarks that are also places of worship (for example a cathedral that is an official stamp) count as landmarks. You can hide any stamp yourself. Private stamps are not shown to other users; our team can see them only in admin tools, for support and safety. Anything you write in a stamp’s note is your own content: it is private unless you choose to show it on your shared passport, and we never use stamps or notes for advertising (the App shows no ads).

9. Children's & Teens' Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction — for example under 16 in Australia). We ask for your date of birth to apply these protections. If the birthday entered shows a user is under that age, we turn off the social features for that account; contact us and we will delete it. Travelers aged 13–17 get additional safeguards: their passports cannot be made public, their stamps reach friends only after a 24-hour delay, new followers require their approval, postcards they send go only to mutual followers, and notes they sign in a place’s public guestbook show only their first name, never their home city. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International Users

We operate from, and use service providers located in, the United States and other countries. By using the App, you understand your information may be processed in countries with different data-protection laws than your own.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections and managed authentication, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
