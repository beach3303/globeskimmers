Privacy Policy

Globeskimmers · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Globeskimmers ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Globeskimmers mobile application and related services (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you create an account or sign in using email, Google, Facebook, or Apple, we collect your name (or first name) and email address to create and secure your account.
  • Travel preferences. During optional onboarding you may share preferences such as your home country, preferred languages, travel budget, accommodation style, favorite countries, and upcoming destinations. These help personalize the App and are optional.
  • Location data. With your permission, we access your device's approximate or precise location to find places near you (restaurants, ATMs, coffee shops, restrooms, stores, and more) and to provide weather and local information. You may instead enter a location manually, and you can disable location access at any time in your device settings.
  • Stamps and your location. A Virtual Passport stamp is earned by being at the place: when you tap Stamp, the App reads your device’s location once and our server checks it against the place’s own location (our records or Google’s). Your position is used only for that check and is not stored; the stamp keeps the place’s location and the date. Other people never see where you stood — only the place’s name and the date, and only for stamps you share.
  • Saved & recent locations. Locations you save or recently used are stored on your device and, where applicable, in your account profile.
  • Past partner-link taps. Before October 3, 2026 the App had links to booking partners; taps on them were recorded with a random click ID (never your name or email). We keep those records for accounting.
  • Website messages. If you write to us through the contact form on globeskimmers.io, we store your name, email, company if you give one, your message, the page you sent it from, a hashed (one-way scrambled) version of your IP address and your browser type. We use them to reply to you and to prevent spam, and the Globeskimmers team reads these messages in our admin tools.
  • Profile & social information. If you use the Virtual Passport's social features, we collect the profile you choose to create: a username (handle), display name, "about me" line, website, favorite places, future destinations, and an optional profile photo. We also collect your birth year (and, optionally, your birthday month and day), to apply age-appropriate protections — see Section 9 — and, only in aggregate (never individually), to understand the age range of the people who use GlobeSkimmers. Your birthday is never shown on your profile or passport.
  • Content you share. The App stores the travel content you create: passport stamps, memory photos, guestbook signatures and finger drawings, postcards, reactions, and comments. Your passport is private by default; stamps, photos and profile details become visible to others only if you make your passport public or share it with people you approve.
  • Device & usage information. We collect general device and usage information (such as app version and which features are used) to operate and improve the App. The App shows no third-party advertising and collects no advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, personalize, and improve the App's features (nearby search, translations, weather, cultural information).
  • Create, authenticate, and maintain your account.
  • Display and measure advertising.
  • Send service-related emails, such as sign-up confirmations.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.

3. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the App:

  • Google — Places, Maps, text-to-speech and translation (for language phrases), and Sign-In. Your use of these features is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account and preferences.
  • Cloudflare — backend infrastructure that processes requests and caches results.
  • Apple and Facebook — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Amazon Web Services (SES) — sends our account-related emails.
  • Resend — sends our emails: invitations you ask us to send, Seal letters, notices to our team about guestbook notes awaiting review, and messages about hotel stays booked in the App before October 3, 2026.
  • Nuitée Travel Ltd — the merchant of record for hotel stays booked in the App before October 3, 2026. For those bookings it holds the guest name, email and stay details it needs to keep the reservation.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

4. Advertising

The App contains no third-party advertising. We do not use advertising SDKs, do not collect or share advertising identifiers, and do not serve personalized ads.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads.

5. No Bookings or Affiliate Links

As of October 3, 2026, the App does not sell or book anything and contains no affiliate or partner booking links. Prices shown in the App — for example admission or parking — are there to help you plan, with their source named; to buy, you go to the venue or its official website yourself.

Bookings made before that date. A small number of hotel stays were booked in the App earlier, sold by Nuitée Travel Ltd as merchant of record (Nuitée charged the card and holds the reservation; Globeskimmers never stored card details). We keep the booking record — hotel, stay dates and booking reference — so we can help with the stay, cancellations and refunds.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see "Your Rights" below). Some information may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your account information by contacting us at the address below.
  • Location: grant or revoke location access in your device settings, or use a manually entered location.
  • Advertising: manage personalized ads via your device settings as described above.

8. User Content, Moderation & Safety

  • Private by default. Your Virtual Passport, its photos and your profile are visible only to you unless you choose to share them.
  • Automated review. Photos, drawings and postcards are reviewed by automated systems before they can be shown to other people; content that fails review is not displayed publicly. Text comments are screened for abuse.
  • Reporting & blocking. Every shared passport, profile, comment and postcard can be reported in the App. Reports are reviewed by our team. You can block any user; blocking is silent and works in both directions. Owners can remove any reaction or comment left on their pages.
  • Removal. To remove content you created, delete it in the App or contact us and we will remove it.

Sensitive places

You can stamp any place you really visit, including places such as hospitals, clinics, schools, shelters and places of worship. The App never asks why you were there. Stamps at places like these start private, visible only to you, and you choose whether to share them. Anything you write in a stamp’s note is your own content: it is private unless you choose to show it on your shared passport, and we never use stamps or notes for advertising (the App shows no ads).

9. Children's & Teens' Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction — for example under 16 in Australia), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them or allow them to use social features. We ask for your birth year to apply these protections. Travelers aged 13–17 get additional safeguards: their passports cannot be made public, new followers require their approval, postcards work only between mutual followers, and their activity is not broadcast publicly. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International Users

We operate from, and use service providers located in, the United States and other countries. By using the App, you understand your information may be processed in countries with different data-protection laws than your own.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections and managed authentication, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.

13. Contact Us

Questions about this Policy or your data? Contact us at founder@globeskimmers.io.
