Child safety standards

Globeskimmers · Developer: Maiza Simeon · Last updated: October 14, 2026

Globeskimmers has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everything people share in the Globeskimmers app: postcards, city board posts, photos, guestbook notes, names and profiles.

What is never allowed

  • Any content that sexualizes a child, including drawn, edited or AI-made images.
  • Grooming: contacting, befriending or pressuring a child for a sexual purpose.
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share intimate images.
  • Trafficking, or offering or asking for a child for any sexual purpose.
  • Sharing, asking for or linking to child sexual abuse material.

How the app keeps children out of social features

  • Every account gives its birthday before it can use anything social or public.
  • An account whose birthday is under 13 is frozen at once, and what it uploaded is deleted within days.
  • Accounts under 18 can't post publicly, send postcards, sign guestbooks or be followed by strangers. They keep the travel tools and a private passport only they can see.
  • Photos and words are checked before they are shared, and content that fails the check never appears.

Reporting a concern

  • In the app: every postcard, city board post, guestbook note, photo and traveler page has a Report option, and you can Block anyone.
  • By email: write to founder@globeskimmers.io with the subject “Child safety”. This is our child safety contact.

What we do

  • We remove content that breaks these standards and close the accounts that post it.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as U.S. law requires, and cooperate with law enforcement.
  • We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us.
If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number first.
